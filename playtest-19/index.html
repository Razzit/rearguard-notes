Patch Notes

PLAYTEST
19

This one is all about how the game looks, sounds and feels. You can see into the shade on a sunny day now, and sandbag walls are built bag by bag. The sun shines through smoke. Helmets fly off on a headshot, and explosions have some weight to them. The gun moves in your hands and the weather changes while you watch. The fight sounds more like a fight, too. We’ve also got rid of the ear-splitting hiss at launch and the glowing dots on the walls.

Light you can see by

Changed

The shady side of a wall used to go black on a sunny day. Now the sunlit ground throws light back into it, like it does in real life.

  • Shade looks like shade, not a hole. Sandbag walls, crates and the post are lit by the ground in front of them on the side away from the sun. It’s real lighting, not a filter, so it works on LOW graphics too.
  • Every front has a lighter, cleaner colour grade. There’s less sepia, softer contrast and a lighter vignette, and the skies are pale and dusty. Looking straight up no longer shows a pinwheel and rings in the sky.
  • The sun shines through smoke. Smoke is lit by the sun and glows at the edges facing it. Haze picks up a warm glow toward the sun, and dust motes catch the light when a shell lands close.
  • Night has its own light. The moon lights the smoke, and fires and lamps light up the smoke near them. Burning wrecks glow brighter after dark. Your muzzle flash lights up your gun and hands for a moment.
  • Sandbag walls are made of bags now. Each one is drawn bag by bag and staggered like a real wall. A breached wall leaves a heap of burst bags. Cover is exactly where it was.

Every hit lands

New
  • Helmets fly off on a headshot kill. That goes for every enemy wearing one, and for your own squadmen.
  • Men fall the way they were hit. A head, chest or leg shot each drops a man differently. A body hits the ground with a thud, and a long shot lands with a distant thwack.
  • Explosions have weight. You get a flash and a fireball, then a ring of dust and clods of earth that bounce. A pall of smoke hangs about afterwards. A shell landing close shakes your view from the direction it hit. The Screen shake slider still controls how much.
  • Muzzle flashes are petal-shaped now, and a wisp of smoke comes off the barrel. Impacts look like whatever they hit. The pale halos round bullet holes are gone.
  • Your own men are more careful with the dead. Corpses lie flat when there’s room, and heads no longer sink into the ground.

The sound of the fight

Changed
  • You hear a close blast properly. When something goes off near you, the music and the background noise of the battle dip for a moment. Then you hear shrapnel and debris patter down.
  • Spent brass rattles where it lands. Distant guns now blend into close ones, without the dip there used to be in between.
  • The Sar’nt-Major and the stings no longer pile on top of each other in a busy wave.
  • No more static. The static in night waves was the flares hissing as they burned, so they don’t hiss any more. We’ve also switched off the wind and the crackle of burning wrecks for now. A flare still pops when it goes up.

The gun in your hands

Changed
  • We rebuilt every gun. They have rounded receivers, real trigger guards and triggers, sling swivels, magazine ribs and stripper clips. The wood is varnished, the steel is blued and the scopes have enamel on them. There’s a new shovel too.
  • Hands are tanned now, and gloved in the Ardennes snow.
  • The gun moves with you. It lags a touch when you turn and sways in a figure of eight as you walk. It trails when you start and stop, and each class of gun kicks at the hip in its own way. Land from a jump and your view dips and rolls. Aiming down the sights feels exactly as before.
  • The slider is now called Weapon sway and bob, under OPTIONS. Set it to 0 to switch all of this off.

Soldiers with faces

New
  • Every soldier has a proper face now, sculpted and painted, with a range of skin tones. Their kit is easy to pick out too: shelter halves, mess tins and webbing.
  • Soldiers are easier to make out close up. Enemies still look dark at a distance, and that’s on purpose.
  • New vehicles. There are GMC and Opel Blitz lorries, a proper Kübelwagen and a more detailed Goliath. The downed pilot on Henderson Field has a body to match, and the anti-tank gun’s loader rams each round home.

Weather that changes in front of you

Changed
  • Night Falls now fades the sky to dark over about 26 seconds, instead of all at once.
  • Dawn Attack starts in the blue hour and breaks into a gold sunrise.
  • Rain splashes on the ground and snow streaks past. Fog rolls in banks.

On the newsreel

New
  • The Last Round cam looks like old film now. It’s warm and washed out, with fine grain, a few scratches and a bit of projector flicker. It also lasts a little longer, so you see the man go down.
  • The shot of the run. When a run ends, its best kills play back as a short reel on every soldier’s screen before the results. Any key skips it.
  • Anti-tank gun kills and tank aces get their own captions. WAVE HELD is now called the moment the shot lands.
  • If you don’t like it, OPTIONS › Newsreel film look turns the film effect off. The Last round camera option still turns the cam off altogether.

The camp and the fronts

Changed
  • The post looks lived in. There’s more detail round the post and the camp, and the flag waves. It’s all for looks. There’s nothing new to trip over or hide behind.
  • New buildings. The Ardennes gets a road and a forester’s lodge, and the Factory District gets a tenement. Monte Lungo’s far town has its colours back, and the Factory has a broken skyline.
  • We audited 1,000 maps for anything out of place. Floating pieces went from 3,759 to 14. There were about 1,900 misplaced items, and now there are none.

Back at HQ

Changed
  • The HQ screens are real paper with worn rubber stamps now. Buttons press and ease when you hover them, and the pages fade from one to the next.
  • You can see the post behind the HQ papers, and the battlefield is much quieter while you’re there.
  • Results captions are bigger and easier to read.

From your playtest 18 reports

Changed
  • What’s new is in the game now. The first time you start a new build, a short card tells you what changed. You can open it again any time from WHAT’S NEW at the top of the HQ.
  • Crack Shot no longer slows the game. You still get the CRACK SHOT banner and its bounty for a run of headshots, but we’ve taken the slow motion out for now. It broke the flow, especially in the quieter early waves.
  • Hand-to-hand kills are now called COLD STEEL and say what did it (a shovel kill says so), instead of always saying BAYONET.
  • Veteran traits are all upside now. We took the drawbacks off.
  • The anti-tank gun sits on a raised platform so it can fire over the camp’s own walls, and it can aim 15° down. It can now hit what’s in front of it on Monte Lungo and Henderson Field.
  • Blow the MG Nest: the satchel is now on the ground outside the post, not on the roof.
  • Night flares no longer blind you. With the brighter nights on top, they were too much.
  • The minimap: paratroopers and snipers used to be drawn green or blue, which are the colours for your own squad and your mates. All enemies are warm colours now, and snipers have a pale dot in the middle.

Fixes

Fixed
  • The loud, static-like hiss when the game first launches is gone. It came from the wind (and the burning wrecks), which are off for now.
  • The static that started a little way into a night wave is gone. It was the flares hissing as they burned.
  • Glowing white dots on walls and planks when you stood close to them are gone. Thanks to everyone who sent screenshots.
  • The Last Round cam and the end-of-run reel move smoothly now, instead of stepping like old film. Each kill on the reel lasts a little longer, so the cuts between them aren’t so jarring.
  • The radio line “Movement on the tree line” no longer says “tree lean”.
  • Fewer stutters. Bodies, guns, vehicles and sounds now load during the opening break, instead of the first time you meet them.
  • Dust motes stay as small specks instead of growing into blobs close to the camera.
  • A fallen man’s head no longer ends up under the ground.

Known issues

  • Bodies still don’t ragdoll. A man falls and settles as one piece.
  • The Trench Gun’s ejection port is a flat black panel up close, and the Garand’s en-bloc clip is a fat cream cylinder.
  • The downed pilot walks about five metres behind his escort, out of view.
  • A squadmate’s body can lie through the camp crates by the wall, and now and then a rifle points straight up from a body.
  • Monte Lungo’s HQ backdrop shows half a house wall.

What we’d like you to test

  • Press F10 the moment something looks or sounds wrong. It sends the map code for you.
  • The sound of a heavy wave: with mortars and artillery coming in, does the dip on close blasts feel punchy, or does it pump and grate?
  • The gun in your hands: is the sway, bob and landing dip too much, too little, or right? Does aiming feel exactly as before?
  • The newsreel: does the end-of-run reel earn its seconds, or is it too long?
  • Weather in motion: any pop, flicker or blinding frame as Night Falls or the dawn comes up?
  • The Factory District facing the sun: does it look milky or washed out?
  • Night, fog and snow: can you read the weapon list and the section roster? Does the plate behind them look like a box?
  • Smoke at night: does a smoke grenade still hide men in the moonlight?
  • In co-op: do helmets and death reactions look the same on both screens, and does the end reel play for everyone?
  • Crack Shot without the slow motion: do you miss it, or is the game better without it?

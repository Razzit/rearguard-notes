The mortar comes with you

New

You asked to move the mortar. Last build it stayed where the plan put it. Now it doesn’t.

  • In a break, hold [F] at the mortar to pick it up. It’s slow going and your rifle is slung, the same as carrying a machine gun. Everyone sees it on your back.
  • Set it on any free mount that takes it, or on clear ground inside the wire. Look at the spot, press [F], turn it with the mouse, then [F] again to confirm.
  • Its bombs, its lay and its health go with it. If one of your hired men was on it, he walks with you and kneels back at the tube when you set it down.
  • Not in a wave, not while it’s knocked out, and not while someone is on it. One gun a soldier. If you leave the game or go down carrying it, it’s set on the nearest free mount.
  • Clients can carry it too. We soaked a game with the host and three clients, one of them moving the mortar every break.
  • You can’t board a lorry with a gun on your back. Set it down first.
  • The AT gun still stays put. That one’s next.

More time, and a card you missed

Changed
  • The break between waves is 30 seconds now. It was 19. Three of you asked for more time. [Z] still starts the next wave early when everyone’s set.
  • At Battle Honours V and up it’s 21 seconds, up from 13.
  • Missed your card in the break? In the fight, tap [Y] and the cards come up and stay up. The number under a card takes it. Tap [Y] again to put them away. One of you got to wave 4 with the cards still waiting and no way to pick one.
  • With the cards up, [U] rerolls them and the NEW CARDS key spends a chit. No need to hold [Y] any more. The old [Y] + number chord still works if that’s what your hands do.
  • While the cards are up, a number key takes a card, not a weapon. Put them away and the numbers are your guns again.

Mounts you can see

New

You said every mounting point should be visible all the time, and more obvious when you’re holding a gun, not just a line of text. Agreed.

  • Every gun mount is a thing on the ground now. A white plate where the gun sits and a board with its name on a stake beside it. F1, F2, the gate leaves, the towers, all of them.
  • Carrying a gun lights every free mount it fits in amber. The one in reach glows brightest. A mount that can’t take what you’re carrying stays dark.
  • While you carry, the HUD lists each free mount with its name and its distance, and puts a diamond on the one you’re at.
  • The engineer’s Portable Mount wears an ENG tag and lights up the same way.

Stand a man down

New

You give your hired men their guns at HQ. You asked to take them off again mid-run. Now you can.

  • Look at your man, or his gun, and tap the squad key [Q]. He leaves the gun and goes back to the line. Tap again at him or the gun to send him back to it.
  • A gun you take its man off stays empty until you send him back. You or anyone else can still man it.
  • If he falls, the man who replaces him waits for your word before taking the gun up.
  • It’s the host’s tap in co-op. A tap anywhere else still pings, and holding the key is still FOLLOW ME.
  • A new deployment clears it. He’s back on the gun the plan gave him.

The lorries

Fixed
  • Riding out on the lorries no longer drops you through the map under the end screen. The lorries stop down the road and you stay aboard. That bug had been there since late October. Thanks for the screenshot with the height on it.
  • Nobody falls out of the world any more. Drop far below where you last stood and you’re put back on your feet.
  • The lorry’s .30 cal turns all the way round its ring. It used to stop 110 degrees either side of the road, with the post in the dead zone, which is why it faced away from the whole fight.
  • Get up on the .30 cal and you face the post and your mates running for the tailgates. Swing it round for the road.

The HQ pages

Fixed
  • The BASE map’s 360 VIEW doesn’t spin on its own any more. It holds still until you turn it. Q and E, or the compass arrows, turn it one step at a time. A held key turns smoothly and stops when you let go. Leave the page and come back and the view is where you left it.
  • You see a gun’s arc before you commit to it. Point at a mount in STARTING MOUNT and the map shows the arc that gun would cover. Point at a Facing arrow and it shows the arc one step round, with your current arc faintly underneath. Drag a gun over a free mount and you see the arc it would have there.
  • HOW HARD no longer piles up. A long Battle Honours list pages now, BACK and MORE, instead of running under HANDICAPS, THE PACT and DONE. It opens on the page with your pick, and a secured tier says so beside its pay.

Hedgerow Farm

Fixed
  • The farmhouse’s front door hangs on its hinges now, open back against the wall. It used to stand out through the wall like a black slab. “This not how door work” was fair.
  • The barn’s broken door hangs from its frame with its corner on the ground.
  • You no longer walk through a knee-high band of stone in the farmhouse doorways.
  • The moving stone on the shelled end wall is fixed. Two pieces of wall sat on the same plane and fought over who drew. The thin shimmering lines at the joints of ruined walls and holed roofs are gone on every front, and stone walls show one continuous pattern instead of restarting every metre.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. It was built between about 10 pm and 4 am and checked with scripted runs, soaks with a host and three clients, and screenshots of the pages. Tell us what we missed.
  • The AT gun can’t be carried yet.
  • On the BASE map you can see a Facing arrow’s arc by pointing at it, but you still click to set it. Dragging the arrow round didn’t make it.
  • At Battle Honours V and VI the last HOW HARD page has an empty band under the rows.
  • With the HUD at 130% and six cards up, the strip overlaps the column on the left.
  • The arc you’ve already set draws faintly under the preview. Over the 360 VIEW’s grass it can be hard to see. Say if it should be stronger.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op in playtest 26. Still not found. If it happens, press F10 at HQ straight away.

What we’d like you to test

  • Carry the mortar. Put it on F2, then on open ground by the wall. Did its man follow? In co-op, let a client carry it.
  • Miss a card on purpose, then tap [Y] in the fight and pick it. Did the strip get in your way? Did a number key fire the wrong thing?
  • Is 30 seconds right? Too long once you know the cards, or still short? Try [Z] to cut it.
  • Find a mount without the HUD. Can you see the plates and boards from across the post? Do the names match the BASE page?
  • Stand a man down and send him back. Did [Q] ping when you meant to release, or the other way round?
  • Ride out on the lorries at the end, and get on the .30 cal on the way. Did you stay on the bed through the telegram?
  • Open UPGRADES > BASE and leave it alone for a minute. Does the view hold still? Then point at mounts and arrows and see if the arcs help.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

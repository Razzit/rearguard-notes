This one is mostly the orders. They used to be marked in the break, so you’d walk over and finish them before the wave turned up. They were quick and a bit of a chore. Now they come in the middle of the fight, with a clock, and what you do about them changes the fight. We also fixed a big memory leak and made a new soldier’s first few visits to HQ calmer.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is.

Orders come during the wave

Changed
  • The break only warns you now. From wave 3, every 3rd or 4th wave, it says “Orders expected during this wave.” You don’t get the job, the place or a marker until it arrives.
  • The order comes once 30 to 50% of the wave has spawned, if the post is at 50% or better. By 70% spawned with no order, none comes, and you’re told. The next wave can try again.
  • A card stands for 8 seconds with the job, its clock, where it is, what a win does, what leaving costs and the pay.
  • Every job has a clock. It fails when the clock runs out. The wave can’t end while the clock runs and someone is on the job, so killing fast doesn’t cost you the order. If nobody’s on it when the wave clears, it fails on the spot.
  • A failed job costs you on the next wave. The cost doesn’t stack, and it never touches your banked pay or ends your run.
  • What each job does now:
  • Mortar Pit (75 s, 21 RP): a win silences the tube. Leave it and it shells you next wave too.
  • Dispatches (75 s, 19 RP): a win means 15% fewer men next wave, and you see what’s in it. Leave it and it’s 15% more.
  • Crossroads (100 s, 28 RP): the hold is 45 s, down from 60. A win stops up to 30% of the men still to come. Leaving it has no cost next wave.
  • Cider Press (60 s, 18 RP): a win means no more grenades this wave. Leave it and it’s 15% more men next wave.
  • Fuel Dump (60 s, 14 RP): a win means no armour next wave. Leave it and any armoured cars and half-tracks come first and early.
  • Sewer Grate (60 s, 14 RP): a win stops the men coming out. Leave it and they keep coming at half rate, this wave and next.
  • Belfry (75 s, 20 RP): a win stops the bombs and any Fire Plan for two waves. Leave it and a new observer drops a bomb every 30 s, until you kill him.
  • Downed Pilot (75 s, 22 RP): a win sends a P-47 down the thickest knot of the wave. Leaving him has no cost next wave.
  • The job comes after you. A hunting party goes for whoever takes the map case or the pilot. Any hit on you during a hold takes the hold back to nothing. The cider press blast really does hurt now: 150 at the charge and 45 at the edge. Enemies close to the pilot can put him down until you raise him.

Forward Observer

New
  • It’s a new job on every front. You go out to a taped ring 32 to 48 m from the post, with 75 s on the clock and 22 RP for a win.
  • Stay in the ring for 30 s to charge a barrage. You can shoot, reload and throw grenades in there. Step out and the charge waits for you. A hunting party sets off 8 s after you step in.
  • A second soldier at the field telephone cuts it to 20 s. It’s on the wall nearest the ring. There’s no key to press. Just stand in the circle and keep fighting. Playing solo, one of your squad goes if one is free.
  • When the guns are laid, ping to aim. It’s your normal ping, Q unless you’ve changed it, from inside the ring. You get a 4 s warning, then 8 shells. Half of them come down on men inside the red ring.
  • It can hurt you and your mates. A shell does 85 to our own, so 1 won’t kill a full-health soldier and 2 can. The post takes nothing, and you can’t aim within 22 m of its middle.
  • Leave the job and it’s 15% more men next wave.

Raids

New
  • A quiet break may ask: TAKE THE RAID? You get 25% more men next wave, and every soldier gets one extra commendation once that wave is held. An order is also certain to come.
  • It’s a section vote: Tab + 1, more than half of you, 10 s to answer. Solo, the ready key says no. If nobody answers, nothing changes.
  • A raid’s job pays half its RP. The prize is the card.
  • You won’t see one in your first run, on HOLD THE LINE, or just before the final assault.

Complications

New
  • After your first 3 orders, a job may draw one of these. It’s a coin flip, and the card tells you which.
  • Sniper: he covers the site. Go for him first, or do the job under his fire.
  • Tripwire: on the map case or a fuel drum, in bright red with a red rag on it. Hold crouched and it takes 1 s longer with no harm. Hold standing and it takes 30 damage, but never your last health.
  • Smoke: a cloud every 12 s over the site. You can’t see through it and nor can they, unless they’re within 6 m.
  • Officer: he stands near the site. Kill him and the next charge is 20% smaller, or 40% with a headshot.

A new soldier’s first few visits

Changed
  • The Front Board and run conditions now open after 2 deployments. The second visit to HQ has less on it. The weekly operation opens a deployment later at rank 1. If you use a same-ground code on your second deployment, it drops its conditions.
  • If all you have is the pistol, NEXT UP tells you to save for the Kar98k and how much RP it takes.
  • On your first MG team wave, the cavalry troop arrives a wave later. That only changes things on Standard. Replacements already holds them back until wave 5.
  • LOADOUT and SERVICE RECORD no longer show padlocks and rows of zeros to a new soldier. Appearance stays hidden until you’ve won something for it.
  • You don’t keep a grade the card never showed you. Grades on older saves still show.

Fixes

Fixed
  • We fixed a memory leak. Each deployment used to add about 90 MB. Now it adds about 2 MB, so long sessions use far less memory. Later deployments also load their guns about 3 times faster.
  • We added safety checks against bad positions and collision shapes, to lower the risk of a rare crash. We didn’t find the cause, so tell us if you crash.
  • F10 reports now say how long your last deployment took to load.
  • While an order’s card is up, other lines in the column stand aside. The clock and the place are the biggest things on it.
  • World marks stay out of the left column. Wave titles sit on a dark band, and name marks stay out of their words. The RP you gain has its own chip.
  • The kill feed no longer runs into the doctrine’s box at HUD 130%.
  • Armour left idle now gives up at 120 s, not 240. Bosses, the train and anything you’ve hurt in the last 30 s keep the 240, so nothing you’re fighting loses its bounty sooner.
  • The Low graphics preset now turns off ambient shadows and sun shafts, and keeps a light colour pass. The Brightness slider works on Low now.
  • If Steam invites can’t open, the game tells you what to do: start from Steam, or share the code. The grey debug line in the lobby is gone.
  • Hosting by direct IP now shows the address friends can reach, on PCs with a virtual network adapter too.
  • The co-op panel now says online play signs you in to Unity’s Relay service anonymously.
  • There are credits now, at RECORDS > Credits and licences. LICENSES.txt is in the game’s folder.
  • Long lines on the after-action card, the Field Commission card and SERVICE RECORD are no longer cut off.
  • Monte Lungo and the Factory: the street lamp is gone, the signs are stencilled and the tower clock has a real face.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, with up to 6 soldiers. The orders have had no real balance testing, so expect some clocks and costs to be off.
  • Jobs may pay a bit too much in a big section. A job pays every soldier in full, so a maxed 6-man section earns more from them than we’d like.
  • The Mortar Pit, Dispatches and Crossroads orders have no voice for now. The old lines described the old jobs. The banner and the card tell you what to do.
  • The cider press blast and your own barrage can kill you when you’re playing solo. We’re not sure that’s right yet, so tell us.
  • Memory still grows by about 2 MB a deployment. We don’t know why yet.
  • Low is about 12% brighter than High, and on our PC it’s slightly slower than the old Low. We haven’t tried it on a weak PC, so tell us how it runs.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op. We still haven’t found the cause. If it happens, please press F10 at HQ straight away, before you restart.
  • The KEPT row on the after-action card can still get cut off.

What we’d like you to test

  • Do the orders feel better? Are they exciting now, or just stressful? Do you go for them, or ignore them?
  • Are the clocks fair? Tell us about any job you couldn’t reach in time, or that was too easy.
  • Run a Forward Observer job with a mate on the telephone. Could you find the telephone? Did the barrage do enough?
  • Take a raid. Was the extra commendation worth 25% more men?
  • Hit a complication. Could you tell what it wanted from you?
  • If you can, start a new soldier. Does the second visit to HQ feel better?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

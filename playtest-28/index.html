PLAYTEST
28

Two rounds of work in one build. The big one is the base. One of you wrote up how you’d want it to work, and most of what’s below is that idea, built. UPGRADES > BASE is a shared arsenal now: six gun slots, five kinds of gun, and the mounts to stand them on, planned on the post itself. In the field you get a 30 second PREPARATION break before wave 1, and you can pick a machine gun up and carry it to another mount. The other round went after the weakest parts of the game we could find: getting pinned, the early waves, the Factory, the cards, the ranks, co-op.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is. Your old base comes across as its guns. If you’d bought MORE POINTS, it’s folded into the 25 and refunded in full, and the save notice says so.

Build your base: the shared arsenal

New
  • Six slots. UPGRADES > BASE opens at rank 9, as before. Each slot takes a gun, an option for it, a mount, a facing, and if you like one of your hired men to work it. You get 16 points at rank 9 and one more a rank, up to 25 at rank 18.
  • Prices. The .30 is free. The .50 is 4, the flamethrower 5, the AT gun 6, the mortar 6. An option on a gun is 2. A wing is 2 and the second tower is 3. One AT gun and one mortar a plan.
  • You plan on the post itself. The 360 VIEW is the 3D post you know. TOP-DOWN is the same post from above. Mounts are dots. Pick a slot, drag a weapon onto a mount, and the gun shows its 180 degree field of fire. The arrows turn it in 15 degree steps. A gun on a gate leaf stays within 45 degrees of its wall.
  • The mounts. The eight gate leaves, the NW watchtower, and two ground mounts inside the wire, F1 and F2. You can buy a wing on any of the four diagonals, and a second watchtower in the SE corner. Towers take machine guns and the flamethrower.
  • The default post. If you never change anything, you have what you had: two guns on the diagonals, on wings we give you free. Change the plan and each wing costs its 2 points.
  • In the field it’s where you put it. Every gun stands on its mount, facing the way you set, on every machine. If a mount is blocked on that front, the gun goes to the next free one. The gateways stay open.
  • In co-op anyone can change the plan. The host’s points and rank decide what’s allowed. Everyone’s page shows the change straight away. BRIEFING’s YOUR BASE card lists the guns before you go.

The guns

New
  • The .30 and the .50. Machine guns as before. The .50 is the heavy one. Either takes a belt as its option: Heavy Ball, AP or incendiary.
  • The 6-pounder AT gun. Stands on any ground mount or wing, stocked from wave 1. Its option is Penetrator rounds, which hit armour 1.5 times as hard. If you don’t put an AT gun on your plan, the front’s own AT gun is still dug in below the gate where it always was.
  • The mounted flamethrower. 8 seconds of flame, then 6 to recharge. It goes on a tower too. Its option is Range.
  • The 3-inch mortar. Sits in a sandbag horseshoe at F1 or F2. Take it with [F] and your bomb lands where your crosshair meets the ground, 25 to 120 m out, three seconds later. Hold the sight to stand up and see over the wall. The ring on the ground is where it will come down. Ten bombs a wave, one every three and a half seconds. The AT crate brings more. It never hurts your section, your squad or the post. Enemy shells can knock it out: hold [F] at it to dig it out.

Carrying a gun

New
  • PREPARATION. From rank 9 the first break is 30 seconds, not 6. Use it to move your guns. If everyone readies up, the wave comes early.
  • Hold [F] at an unmanned machine gun to pick it up. A tap still mans it. Carrying is slower going, your rifle is slung, and you can’t sprint, jump or crouch. The free mounts are marked.
  • Setting it down. At a free mount press [F]. Turn it with the mouse in 15 degree steps, then [F] again to confirm. On a gate leaf it stays within 45 degrees of the wall. Anywhere else the prompt says “Only on a mount”.
  • Nothing is picked up in the fight. If a wave starts while you’re carrying, you can finish setting it down, and that’s all.
  • In co-op the first to grab a gun has it. Everyone sees it on his back. A soldier who leaves the game with one drops it on the nearest free mount.
  • The AT gun and the mortar stay where they are this build. Hold [F] at either and it tells you so. Carrying the heavy pieces is next.
  • Engineers get a Portable Mount. It’s free. In a break, set it up where you’re looking, then carry any machine gun onto it.

Your hired men

New
  • Name a man on a gun and he mans it from wave 1. He stays on it through the breaks. If you take the gun he steps aside, and he’s back on it when you leave. The roster names the gun he’s on.
  • He follows a carried gun. Pick up his gun and he walks with you, and he’s back on it a few seconds after you set it down.
  • He works the mortar himself. A man on the mortar drops bombs on enemy mortar teams first, dead ground included, then on any knot of three or more men. He steps off for you, for an empty crate, or for a revive.

The fight

Changed

After playtest 27 went out we spent a night finding the weakest parts of the game we could and reworking them. Fourteen areas, each checked by a second pair of eyes before it went in. These are the ones you’ll meet in the field.

  • Getting pinned. The ring shows where the fire comes from, and the block says it: PINNED FROM THE EAST. Get down behind the bags, not a gateway, and the rounds stop and your health comes back. If you die, the debrief names what killed you.
  • A mortar in dead ground. From wave 7 to 9 a mortar team can set up where the wall can’t shoot it. Walk out round its flank, grenade it or call the guns. Or give your own mortar to a hired man. He’ll answer it, and in our runs he put a man of that team down in 5 to 12 seconds. Tell us if that’s too strong.
  • A fresh soldier’s early waves. Wave 1 starts sooner and has a kill in its first minute. Wave 4’s charge is announced and smaller, and its grenades can’t one-shot you. A lone armoured car is no longer the whole run. At four to six soldiers the charges come from two sides.
  • A bolt-rifle headshot kills an ordinary man on any wave, at any section size. The Carbine, the Enfield and the Trench Gun each got a job. Blasts scale with the man’s health. Your mate’s rounds show on the man you’re both shooting.
  • The HUD. A sapper inside 35 m is marked on every screen. The post bar names the wall under attack. The hurt wash is lighter.
  • The Factory is a street fight now. Broken house fronts 30 to 60 m out, for you and for them. It’s slower and grindier. The Ardennes gets thickets and felled trunks, and the wagon’s door is a real hole you can shoot through.
  • Each front wears its front. The post and the ring are dressed for the map. Monte Lungo has its far hills and the Factory its skyline.
  • Downed men crawl. His mates read his clock. A last-stand kill buys him 5 seconds.

Ranks, cards and the section

Changed
  • Rank 2 lands on the first after-action card. The rank curve is shorter at the bottom. Every promotion names what it brings, and the second climb has a goal of its own.
  • Doctrines from rank 2, not 10. Patience and Opening Burst pay in a fight. Beaten Zone pins. The Feed Tray feeds a real belt of 150 a wave.
  • Gun mastery is twice as fast, and you can fit two parts to a slot from mastery 6.
  • Card suits are sets you count. A card says DEMOLITION 1/3 and the bonus is written on it. A copy says it’s a copy. A reroll gives you fresh cards. Every early offer has two cards that change how you fight.
  • The telegram says what you kept and what’s next. HOW HARD says each thing once. Esc closes things.
  • Co-op. A higher-ranked friend’s rank opens the section’s questions, PUSH ON? and the dare. The section has a best wave to beat. Three co-op cards, Side by Side, Pass the Word and Carry Him, reach you through the dossier.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, soaks with a host and three clients, and screenshots of every page at two sizes. Two rounds of this size will have things we didn’t see. Tell us the page, or the wave, and send a screenshot.
  • The AT gun and the mortar can’t be carried yet.
  • On your mates’ screens a man carrying a gun still holds his rifle. On your own it’s slung.
  • The mortar has no spoken “Shot!” yet. You get SHOT! on the screen, the thump and the whistle.
  • On TOP-DOWN the SE TOWER and NW TOWER labels can sit partly under a tag.
  • A crewed mortar may be too strong against the dead-ground team. See above, and tell us.
  • The Factory plays slower now. If it’s a slog, say so.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op in playtest 26. We still haven’t found the cause. If it happens, press F10 at HQ straight away.

What we’d like you to test

  • Plan a base. If you’re rank 9 or more, go to UPGRADES > BASE and try the .50, the mortar and a wing. Did the page make sense? Did the guns stand where you put them?
  • Give a hired man a gun, and the mortar if you have one. Did he do what you expected?
  • Use the PREPARATION break. Carry a gun to another mount. In co-op, two of you go for the same gun.
  • Host a game and let a client change the plan. Did everyone see it? Did the fight match the page?
  • Get pinned on purpose. Does the ring tell you where it’s coming from? Does getting down work?
  • Play the Factory, and a wave 7 to 9 with the mortar team in dead ground, with and without a mortar of your own.
  • If you can, start a new soldier. Is wave 4 fair now? Does rank 2 on the first card feel right?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

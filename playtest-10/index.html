Latest notes

Co-op chat

New
  • There’s a telephone log in the lobby, and chat fades out in the field.
  • Chat can be read aloud if you want (it’s off by default).

Downed and threat markers

New
  • A downed teammate shows a big medic’s cross with DOWN, their callsign and range. If they’re off screen it sits on the edge with an arrow. An alert crosses the HUD when someone goes down.
  • Announced attacks get a marker showing where they’re coming from, on screen and on the compass.
  • The revive bar goes the moment the revive completes.

Kill streaks

Changed

The ladder keeps climbing now: multi 4, mega 5, ultra 6, monster 8, rampage 10, unstoppable 12, then a step every two after that. Decorations count a streak once, however high it goes.

Gun feel

Changed
  • Muzzle flash draws fire. Shooting gives your position away.
  • Mad Minute keeps the iron sights on target.
  • Shoulder Charge bowls through a crowd.

Monte Lungo

New

Climb the bell tower and go into the houses by the east gate. Enemies sent after you take a spot that can actually see you.

Fixes

Fixed
  • The telegram fits six soldiers. The roll call sits beside it or goes compact.
  • There’s a COPY button beside the join code, and it says COPIED when you use it.
  • Personal kit opens without a freeze.
  • The host can reset Battle Honours through the game.
  • Clouds drift slowly downwind.
  • Kill streaks only count once toward decorations.

What we asked you to test

  • Do the downed markers make reviving easier in a six-player section?
  • Muzzle flash drawing fire: fair, or too punishing for the MG?
  • The bell tower: too strong a spot?

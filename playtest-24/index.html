Patch Notes

PLAYTEST
24

This one is about your first hour and your reports. The game now tells you what you get next. An early loss doesn’t stamp a D on you. Every run has a high point. After a run with friends, you can be back in the fight in under 30 seconds. And three things you told us about from playtest 23 are done: E works again after you ready up, the Sniper’s Veil hides you from machine guns and vehicles, and near misses knock your aim less.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is.

Your reports

Fixed
  • E works again after you ready up. This was our bug, from playtest 23’s “one press does one thing”. A ready tap held on to the key and never let it go. After that, every press of E missed your kit and the post repair until you restarted the game, and only a ready press got through. The hold now ends when you let go of the key. The press that readies you and starts the wave still does nothing else.
  • The Sniper’s Veil hides you from machine guns and vehicles. Hidden under the veil, you can only be picked out from inside 12 m. That now goes for dug-in MG42s, the machine guns on armoured cars, half-tracks and tanks, the flak on the train, and the main guns on tanks and the train. A gun already firing on you lets go when you hide.
  • The men you hit don’t find you under it either. Men you shoot from under the veil, and the squads hunting you, no longer pick you out past 12 m. No new hunt turns on you while you stay hidden.
  • The veil’s rules are the same. Stand up, walk off or fire a shot that isn’t a headshot and it’s gone. Inside 12 m you can still be seen.
  • The gunsmith’s padlocks say “Mastery 2”, not “Weapon level 2”. Attachments and camo open at mastery, not the gun level you buy with RP, so a level 3 pistol with mastery 1 looked like a bug. Only the words changed.

Suppression

Changed
  • You told us it felt endless, and that past a point you couldn’t hit anything. We measured it. Being pinned never touched your spread, sway or recoil. The one thing it did to your aim was a small kick to the view for every round that passed close. In a late wave that’s several rounds a second.
  • Enemies fire just as much as before. Nothing here changes how often they shoot, how many there are or how hard they hit. Every round still whizzes, cracks and kicks up dust.
  • The kick fades as you get more pinned. The first round past your ear kicks nearly as hard as it always did. By the time you’re fully pinned, each round kicks a quarter of that. So cover, a friend, a medic, Steady Under Fire and kills now steady your aim as well as your screen.
  • On the sights the kick is half, and it settles twice as fast. From the hip it settles as before.
  • The suppressed screen is lighter. The brown vignette, the washed-out colour, the colour fringes and the pale haze now show at 0.5 of their full strength when you’re fully pinned. It was 0.8. The muffled sound and the STEADYING bar haven’t changed.
  • It isn’t gone. The screen is lighter, but it is still on for most of a hard wave. When bots played a late wave alone, it was on for about 8 in 10 seconds, the same as before. We haven’t made it end sooner. We made it cost you less while it’s on.
  • What that did for bots, firing a perfectly aimed Kar98k in a late wave, in the moments they hadn’t just been hit: a chest at 60 m went from 76% to 89%, and a chest at 100 m from 67% to 82%. A bot doesn’t flinch or get annoyed, so only you can tell us if it feels better.
  • Being hit still jolts your view much harder than any near miss, and blasts still shake it. Those didn’t change.

Next up

New
  • NEXT UP shows what you get next. Up to three lines: your next rank, your next buy and your next gun or class reward. You’ll see it on the after-action report, beside your XP bar at HQ, on BRIEFING from day one, and under “Wave held” in a break.
  • An early loss no longer stamps a D on you. That goes for every loss at rank 1, and your first loss up to rank 9. The report shows what you kept and how close the next rank is. At rank 1 there’s no break stamp and no wave-by-wave grades either. A rank 1 soldier who wins still gets the overall stamp.
  • Your first deployment always unlocks something. Your first requisition slip is free when you get back.
  • Each run shows how long you held: on the telegram, on the report, and as “about N min a run” on the FRONTS sheet.
  • The Quiet Front pays full RP below rank 3. It paid 85%, shown in red, to the soldiers who could least afford it. The waves are as easy as they were.
  • The “deployments to the next rank” estimate uses your own average now. It doesn’t guess before your first deployment, and it stops once you’re past 5.

First runs

New
  • In your first two deployments, messages come one at a time. Nothing is skipped. They wait their turn. No field manual card shows in the first 10 seconds of a wave.
  • The first break is in order. The banner, then the card pick, then anything left over.
  • Your first run on Hedgerow Farm has a named wave 3: MG TEAM ON THE ROAD. It has its own brief, and a short card says who knocked the team out.
  • Shoot the loader first, on every front. Kill an MG team’s loader and the gun stops for 5 seconds. The gunner fires more slowly after that.

Every run peaks

New
  • Short fronts deal big cards sooner. On Hedgerow Farm, Legendaries can turn up from wave 3, and by wave 8 the odds are as good as they get.
  • Halfway through every front, one card in the break’s offer is Epic or Legendary. It’s still three cards and one pick.
  • Knock out the ace at the relief and he drops a card. Pick one of three before you choose whether to hold the line. It doesn’t use up a Legendary place. Everyone in the section gets their own three.

Together

Co-op
  • After a run with friends, press READY FOR ANOTHER on your report. The host sees who’s in and presses DEPLOY AGAIN: same front, same orders, new map. In our test runs it took 22 to 24 s from the report to wave 1 with everyone ready. LEAVE is still there, just smaller.
  • New to the war in a friend’s game? You’re lent a Kar98k, and a class your section is missing, for that run. YOUR KIT says “(loaned)”. Buy your own at HQ. Don’t want them? Press NO THANKS. What you earn with them goes on your records.
  • Everyone needs playtest 24. The report page takes ready ticks now, so the network code changed. The game turns an older build away on join.
  • Join codes work as they did.

The HQ

Changed
  • UPGRADES is one simple list until rank 13. What you can buy, what it does and the price. No more empty columns. Things that open within the next two ranks show with their rank, and nothing further off does. Rest the mouse on a row to read it in full.
  • RECORDS only shows a page once there’s something on it.
  • If Rearguard closes unexpectedly, it asks once at the next start whether to send a report. SEND REPORT sends the end of that session’s log, its errors, the build and your system to our Discord. Chat and addresses are left out. DON’T SEND sends nothing, and nothing goes unless you choose.
  • F10 reports now also name the first error the game hit in your session.

Fixes

Fixed
  • The field HUD no longer shows through at the edges of the after-action report.
  • REVIVES and MEDALS no longer run together on a win’s report.
  • NEXT UP only says “requisition a rifle” when you have the RP. Otherwise it says how much you’re short.
  • The telegram’s spacing, “1 WAVE HELD”, and commas in big numbers on the telegram and the citation.
  • “Battle Honours I-III and 2 more” reads the way it should.

Known issues

  • With the HUD at 130%, the prompt for placing works runs a little past its box. It did in playtest 23 as well.
  • The brief blur when a round passes close hasn’t changed. It can smear men far off for a moment. We don’t know how much that costs you on a real screen, so tell us.
  • BRIEFING’s PLAY WITH FRIENDS panel has a small grey “Steam off” line in this build. It’s for our own testing. Ignore it. Join codes work without Steam.
  • BRIEFING’s soldier holds a rifle even when you only have a pistol.
  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, including 6 soldiers at once. The numbers in these notes come from bots.

What we’d like you to test

  • Play a late wave under heavy fire. Can you hit things now? Does the screen still say “you’re under fire”? Does it still feel endless?
  • When you can’t hit anything, what’s the cause? Is it the sights jumping, the screen going brown, or being hit? Only you can split those.
  • Sit under a fresh MG42. Does the first burst still make you duck?
  • Press E a lot. Ready up, then use your kit and repair the post, over several waves and a second deployment. Did a press ever go missing?
  • Hide under the veil past 12 m from a machine gun or a tank. Did it ever pick you out?
  • Lose a run early. Did you want to go again? Did NEXT UP tell you something you wanted?
  • Play with friends and press READY FOR ANOTHER. Did everyone know what was happening?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

From the sky

Paratroop drop

Infiltrators land behind you and press on to the post.

Weapon attachments

New

Buy attachments with requisition at HQ and fit them slot by slot. They show on your viewmodel and on the gun your teammates see.

Co-op rewards

Changed

Playing together should never feel like competing for kills.

  • Bounties are split by the damage each soldier did, not who landed the last shot.
  • 30% of every bounty goes to a section pool everyone shares.
  • Revives, resupply and covering fire all earn requisition now.
  • The results have a team roll call and team decorations.

Iron sights and last stand

Fixed
  • Cleaner iron sights. The StG 44 sits further from the eye, and the Garand’s sight line clears the clip.
  • When you go down in co-op you draw an M1911 for a last stand.

What we asked you to test

  • Mortars: can you find them? Should they be easier to see?
  • Co-op pay: does the split feel fair?
  • Attachments: which ones did you actually buy?

Patch Notes

PLAYTEST
23

This one is fixes from playtest 22. Most came from your reports and from playing it ourselves. Double Tap does what it says now. The B.A.R. looks like a B.A.R. down the sights. Machine gunners are easier to hit over their shields. One press of E does one thing. The grey “lakes” on the fields are gone. And we went through every bit of text in the game and took out the riddles. The guns play as they did in playtest 22.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. A playtest 22 build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is.

Guns

Changed
  • Double Tap keeps your scope up. When a bolt-action round hits a man and doesn’t kill him, the perk works the bolt for you. It always readied the next round. But the sights still dropped off your eye for the whole bolt stroke, so with a scope you were back on target no sooner than without the perk. Now the sights stay put and you can fire again straight away. Iron sights had a smaller dip, and that’s fixed too.
  • The B.A.R. has its real sights. The old ones were a bare ring on a stick and a thin post sticking out of the flash hider. Now it’s the M1918A2’s. There’s a rear leaf on the receiver with a peep, guards either side and a windage knob, and a front blade under a hood. We built it from the 1943 field manual. The peep is bigger than the real one so you can see through it. Where you aim is where the rounds go, same as before.
  • The Aperture Sight attachment on the B.A.R. now gives the same rear sight with a smaller peep. It used to add a second ring floating behind the first.
  • No damage, magazine, reload or recoil numbers changed.

The enemy

Changed
  • Machine gunners show more head. Their gun shield is 7 cm lower at the top, so the helmet and brow clear it. Face and chest stay behind the plate. At 60 m you can hit more than twice as much of his head. With a steady aim, your chance of a headshot goes from about 1 in 5 to about 2 in 5. You said they were too hard to hit while they were suppressing you. This is for iron sights as much as scopes.
  • You get told when the post is left alone. On a counter-attack objective, the rest of the wave still goes for the post. That’s the choice the objective asks you to make. But nobody told you. Now, if the post is taking hits and nobody is within 19 m of it, everyone sees “The post is unguarded and under attack - get back to it”. It won’t say it more than once every 25 s.

Plain words

Changed
  • We took the riddles out. A lot of the game’s text read like a puzzle. “A tally of 5, built in the fight, never refilled.” “A notch on the stock for every clean kill.” You told us it read like a machine wrote it, and you were right. We went through every menu, card, perk, kit, upgrade, prompt and message. About 270 lines now just say what the thing does.
  • The kit captions are gone. Those were the little lines next to each class kit’s name. They didn’t say anything the description didn’t. The row shows the charges in plain words now, like “Builds to 5 in a fight”.
  • We kept the names. Perks, kits and STEADY, LADS! are what they were. The announcer and the letters still sound like 1944.
  • We tried not to make anything longer. Where a rewrite grew, we cut it back.

The HQ

Changed
  • Each part of the BRIEFING page looks different now. A tester asked for this after playtest 21, so you can see at a glance where your kit is and where your base is. MISSION and DISPATCHES are olive drab, with a pennant and envelopes. YOUR KIT is webbing tan, with a soldier carrying what you’ve picked. Empty slots are dashed, and the card under your pointer lights up its part of him. YOUR BASE (it was THE MAP) is slate, with the base’s slots numbered on the sketch and listed beside it. Red is still only for DEPLOY and MARK READY.
  • The Specialist level rows show everything. On LOADOUT › SPECIALIST most rows were folded down to names. You had to click twice to pick, and long descriptions ran into the next row. Now every option shows in full. The one you’re using has a filled box and says IN USE. The one a click would take has an empty box. A row you haven’t reached says why. If it doesn’t all fit, it scrolls instead of cutting text off.
  • The level rewards moved under the class list, with every name written out. They used to trail off with “…”.

Service stars

Changed
  • The souvenir guns are gone. Re-enlisting used to leave an MP40 and a scoped Kar98k on crates at the post. Nobody picked them up, because you already deploy with the guns you chose. So the crates and their labels are gone.
  • Service Star III gives you an extra commendation instead. Every deployment, your first commendation offer comes with a second one straight after it. Not in training. Star I still gives your service chevrons.
  • Your save loads as it is. The souvenirs were never saved, so you haven’t lost anything.

Together

Co-op
  • Everyone needs playtest 23. Your own game works out your hits, and the gun shield changed shape. A playtest 22 player in a 23 game would be shooting at a different shield from everyone else. So the game turns a 22 build away on join.
  • We ran it with 6 soldiers. The extra commendation, the warning about the post and the ready tap work the same for every player.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Tapping E to ready up could also put up the engineer’s Sandbag Pack wall. If your tap was the one that started the wave, the game saw E pressed in a wave on the same frame. One press does one thing now. In a break the kit row says “SANDBAG PACK [E in a wave]” and the ready line says “[Tap E] ready up”. Holding E still repairs the post or plants the Aid Post.
  • Grey “lakes” on the fields that vanished when you looked at them. They weren’t water. They were the flat slab we put under the whole map for the far horizon, showing through every dip in the rolling ground. It sat so close to the ground that it flickered in and out as you turned. The horizon is a ring around the field now, so there’s nothing under your feet to show through. Hedgerow Farm, the Ardennes and the Factory all had it.
  • The Chain Reaction card’s text didn’t fit on the card. It does now.

Known issues

  • With the HUD at 130%, the prompt for placing works runs a little past its box. It did in playtest 22 as well.
  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, including 6 soldiers at once. The gun shield height and the rewritten text are the most likely to change after you’ve played.

What we’d like you to test

  • Take Double Tap on a scoped rifle. Hit a man without killing him. Can you put the second round in before he moves? Is the card worth taking now?
  • Look down the B.A.R.’s sights. Does it look right? Is the peep too big, too small, or in the way?
  • Take on a machine gun nest with iron sights. Can you get the headshot while he’s shooting at you? Is it too easy now?
  • Read the cards, perks and kits. Did you have to read anything twice? Tell us the exact line.
  • Did the BRIEFING page make it obvious where your kit and your base are?
  • Engineers, tap E to ready up a few times. Did a wall ever go up when you didn’t want one?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

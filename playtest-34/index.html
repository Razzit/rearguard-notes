Across Sunday night and Monday morning Lachie sent a crowd-control grenade idea in three parts and eight field reports from his first runs on Playtest 33. This build has all of them. Each one was traced in the code and measured before anything changed: the gunner clubbed from behind, the sixth grenade, the dawn into the sun.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is.

Pick your grenade

New

Lachie asked for crowd control in the late waves. From rank 10, the GRENADES slot on the SOLDIER page lets you carry one type for the whole pouch.

  • FRAG, as before.
  • STUN. Every man within 9 metres stops, goes to ground and shoots at half his aim for five seconds, stumbling while he is dazed. Nobody dies. A machine-gun crew falls silent for those five seconds. Vehicles shrug it off.
  • MOLOTOV. A patch of fire the size of a frag’s burst that burns for five seconds. On our wave-8 test it killed as many as a frag did, nine of twelve, all within about a second. Its kills count as flame kills.
  • GAS. A yellow-green cloud nine metres across that lasts ten seconds. It hurts slowly, and every man in it loses his nerve the way he does when his officer falls. Any fire sets it off in a fireball: a blast in it, a flamethrower across it, a molotov’s patch, a burning man. The enemy’s fire sets it off too. A fireball at the post hurts you at half, like a frag.
  • Bundled Charges and Fire Bombs are frag cards, so you won’t be dealt them while you carry another type.
  • The GRENADES card adds up. Lachie carried six and the card said five. The sixth was real: it comes from the rank-34 bonus, which a Service Star opens early. The card now lists every source of your pouch, so the number matches.

In the field

Fixed
  • Your men turn on an enemy inside the wire. A man on a gun never turned round, so one shock trooper could walk the line clubbing gunners from behind, about 20 seconds a man. Now a man struck inside the post’s ring turns and fights back, the man beside him turns too, and both go back to their guns when the attacker is down. In the same setup he dies in under three seconds, having struck one man. Enemies outside the ring are handled as before.
  • A downed man wears the medic’s cross, with his name and his clock (“DOWN 24 s”), out to 90 metres. A downed man and a dead one used to look exactly the same.
  • The dawn attack no longer washes the skyline white when you face the sun. Dawn was overriding each front’s tuned glow with a stronger one. Facing the sun, the horizon is about 15% darker and the ruins and trees stand out against it again.
  • The weapons rack takes two of the same gun, so the crate’s second bazooka hangs beside the first.

At the HQ

Changed
  • Every perk and trait on one page. Open PERKS and the list takes the soldier’s space in three columns, where it used to run to three or four pages. “‹ THE SOLDIER” takes you back.
  • The Battalion HQ’s TRAITS page is gone. Traits are worn from PERKS now. The Battalion HQ holds STORES and FUND.
  • Pick lists open on what you have fitted, carried or worn, framed in amber with its tag. They used to open on the next thing to buy.
  • The Emergency Flare cache, 30,000 RP. This is the STORES cache that was called Fire Plan. Lachie was right that one early fire mission does little; what the cache really buys is the flare’s rule, and the row now says so. If you picked it at 50,000, unpicking returns 30,000.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. It was checked with scripted runs and four-player soaks. Tell us what we missed.
  • In daylight the STUN’s flash reads as a puff of dust, the molotov’s edge is hard to see, and a grenade in flight is too small to follow.
  • A player who joins mid-run does not see a gas cloud that is already standing.
  • The Improvised Mine still stakes a frag, whatever you carry.

What we’d like you to test

  • Carry each grenade for a run. Which would you pick at wave 12? Does any of them break the late waves?
  • Throw a molotov into a gas cloud.
  • Go off on an order and leave your men on the guns. Does anyone get through the wire now?
  • Lose a man and find him by the cross.
  • Play a dawn attack facing the sun.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

Training range

New

An optional solo boot camp. 12 guided drills cover aiming, grenades, the nests, artillery and reviving.

A score

New

The game has procedural music now. There’s a march at HQ and something calmer for the intermission. The combat music is layered and builds with the wave, and the final assault gets its own theme. There are stingers too.

Controls

Changed
  • Crouch and Sprint can toggle now. It’s in Options, and the default is HOLD.
  • Ready up with a tap of E (Enter also works). Engineers still repair by holding E.
  • Picking a commendation or wagon stock with a number key no longer switches your weapon as well.

In the field

Changed
  • Holding a wave tops up every soldier’s magazines at once.
  • Grenades bounce less and stay near where they land.
  • The binoculars raise and lower smoothly.
  • The quartermaster’s wagon stands out in the wave break.

What we asked you to test

  • Do the three new guns have a reason to be picked over the old five?
  • New players: does the training range get you ready for wave 1?

Your hired men

New

You asked for more men, for commands, and for a way to get them back up. Assign, unassign, follow me and hold here were already in. The rest is here.

  • Hire up to six. Pvt. Delgado with a Garand at rank 22 for 7,500 RP, and Cpl. Haskell with a B.A.R. at rank 25 for 12,000. Marsh, the fourth man, now opens at rank 18 instead of 25. The squad page shows five or six in two rows.
  • Six on the line in co-op. The host’s men go first, then one from each friend. If your mates bring nobody, all six of yours come.
  • Anyone can put a man on a gun. On UPGRADES > BASE a client can now assign one of the host’s men to a slot. The host checks it.
  • Double-tap [Q] and they come home. While your men follow you or hold outside the wire, two quick taps of [Q] send them back to their posts. One tap still pings. The order line on the roster says so.
  • A hired man goes down instead of dying. Hit to zero, he lies there for 30 seconds with his rifle slung. Rounds pass him and the enemy look past him. Hold [F] beside him for 3 seconds and he’s up on half health. A medic does it in 1.5 seconds and gets him up on 90%. A syrette holds his clock. If nobody gets to him he’s gone and replaced after the wave as before. If you hold the wave, every man still down gets up.
  • A blast that takes a standing man to zero downs him, and nothing hurts a man already down. That’s the rule you play by, so your men do too.
  • Not built yet: the men picking each other up. Say if you want it.

The approach map

New

You asked for a map of the upcoming spawns to plan against. The game doesn’t know every spawn in advance, it rolls each squad’s edge as it comes. What it does know in a break is where most of the next wave is coming from. So that’s what the map shows.

  • In a break, hold [M]. A paper sheet of the post from above, the sixteen edges lettered. The edges the main thrust comes from are red with arrows in, and it says MOST FROM THE NORTH-WEST, or FROM ANY SIDE when there is no thrust yet.
  • Under it, the lane intel line and the name of the event. Your guns are amber dots, your section white ones. When the intel names armour, the road says ARMOUR.
  • The first three breaks remind you of the key, then it’s in the field manual. You can rebind it under CONTROLS.
  • Nothing shows in the fight. The compass wedge is still the fight’s picture.
  • While doing this we found the thrust could stray. About one charge in 20 came from an edge the map wouldn’t have marked. Now a charge on the thrust always comes from a marked edge.

Explosives late in a run

Changed

All five points you listed.

  • Fewer grenades from wave 8. The ordinary riflemen’s shared grenade clock slows from wave 8, down to half as many by wave 14. The GRENADIER veteran keeps his own. In our wave 14 soak the throws a minute went from 4 to 7 down to 0 to 2.
  • Grab an enemy grenade from 6 metres. That was Hot Potato’s reach. It’s everyone’s now. Hot Potato still means back at once, further and harder.
  • A landed grenade burns 2.4 seconds, not 1.4. Thrown back from 12 metres it now bursts half a metre from the man who threw it. It used to fall 7 metres short.
  • Enemy mortars and rockets land twice as wide, for everyone. That’s what Radio Jamming used to buy. The dead-ground mortar team’s bombs on the post are the exception, so that fight keeps its clock.
  • Crews slow as they lose men. A rocket crew’s salvo gap doubles with one man gone and quadruples with two. A lone mortarman fires every 15 seconds instead of 11.

Suppression late in a run

Changed

All five points. You were right that the earlier fix never touched the first round.

  • The first kick is smaller, 0.5 degrees instead of 0.8, and it takes four rounds to pin you instead of three.
  • At a mounted gun you shake it off as fast as behind the bags. The kick is halved again there, the HUD says AT THE GUN, and it stops telling you to get down.
  • MG42 gunners show more head. Eyes and brow over the shield where only the helmet showed.
  • A nest that loses its loader goes quiet. The gunner loads every belt himself and the gun fires about 40% of the time. If the gunner dies first, the loader who takes over is alone too.
  • Blasts rock your view half as much, clear sooner, and reach less far. The damage is the same.
  • Measured on the same seeds against playtest 29: at wave 12 on the ruins, chest hits at 60 metres went from 65% to 72% and time pinned from 76% to 64%.

Leaves hide you

Fixed
  • The officer who shot you through the corner of a bush. Bushes have never had a collider, by design, and the enemy’s sight check was a plain ray, so a man behind leaves could pick you out while you couldn’t see him. Now enemies ask the same rule the hidden spawns use, and a hedgerow’s end hides you too.
  • Your hired men don’t fire through leaves either, and nor do your crewed nests.
  • Rounds still pass through leaves. A bush is concealment, not cover.
  • Armoured car gunners, enemy MG teams and cavalry still use the plain ray. Tell us if they catch you through a hedge.

Points and cards

Changed
  • 35 arsenal points at rank 18. 26 at rank 9, one more a rank. You asked for it to test more combinations, and 35 fits every heavy gun with its option and a second .50. Your playtest 29 plan loads as it was with 10 to spare. If 35 turns out to be the right game, say so.
  • Chain Reaction and Shock Rounds once every 3 seconds. Chain Reaction’s belt was firing once a second and, with Bundled Charges, feeding itself. Shock Rounds had no cooldown at all. Both cards now say Every 3 s on their face. The blue tracer only shows on a round that can arc.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. It was built on Friday afternoon from your reports and checked with scripted runs and a four-player soak to wave 10. Tell us what we missed.
  • A hired man who is down when the lorries leave is left behind.
  • The approach map, held in the first break, covers the end of the mission title, and the recon strip on small screens.
  • The same map code rolls its waves differently from playtest 29, because of the thrust fix.
  • The AT gun still can’t be carried.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op in playtest 26. Still not found. If it happens, press F10 at HQ straight away.

What we’d like you to test

  • Hire six and lose one. Let a man go down and get him up with [F]. Try it as the medic. Try it as a client.
  • Take the men out with FOLLOW ME, then double-tap [Q]. Did they come straight back? Did a single tap still ping?
  • Hold [M] in every break. Did the red side match where most of them came from? Could you read it at your screen size?
  • Play to wave 12 or more. Are the grenades, the barrages and the suppression bearable now, or did we go too far?
  • Stand at a mounted gun under fire. Does AT THE GUN feel different?
  • Hide behind a bush and see whether anyone still finds you through it.
  • Spend 35 points. Is there a plan you still can’t make?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

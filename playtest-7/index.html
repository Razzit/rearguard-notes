Latest notes

Battle Honours

New

Secure a front to open the next tier on it. Each tier gives the enemy more health and damage and sends more of them. There are more officers and heavies, and specials come earlier. At the top there’s a second Panzer.

Tiers I–III

Rank 10

Tougher, but it pays more: +15% RP and +10% XP per tier.

Tiers IV–VI

Rank 20

Specials arrive early, and heavies come in numbers.

Tiers VII–X

Rank 30

Tier X has an event every wave. You earn battle stars, camo at V and X, and an insignia at X.

In co-op the section deploys at the lowest tier everyone has open.

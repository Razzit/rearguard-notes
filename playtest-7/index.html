Latest notes

Battle Honours

New

Secure a front to open the next tier on it. Each tier adds health, damage, numbers, officers, early specials, heavies, and at the top a second Panzer.

Tiers I–III

Rank 10

Tougher, and pays more: +15% RP and +10% XP per tier.

Tiers IV–VI

Rank 20

Specials arrive early. Heavies in numbers.

Tiers VII–X

Rank 30

An event every wave at X. Battle stars, camo at V and X, and an insignia at X.

In co-op the section deploys at the lowest tier everyone has open.

Patch Notes

What’s changed in each playtest build of Rearguard, our first-person WW2 roguelite base defence. Newest at the top.

Latest

Playtest 21

This one is about reading the game, not changing it. The BRIEFING is four fields with one figure for what the run pays. Every card says what it does in nine words or fewer, and a tap of [Y] shows the full rule. A break asks one question at most: hold Tab and tap 1 or 2. The works build themselves every third break, so K, J and L are gone and E only means READY. Recut on 27 October after the first session. The HUD in a fight only shows what you can act on, breaks are 19 s and you can set your own crosshair. Shoot at the enemy from outside the wire and they’ll come for you. The ace is a boss now, a King Tiger cuts the road to the lorries, and you have to get into the lorries yourself. Plus fixes to the range, the after-action card and names, and more.

Playtest 20

Your class and your RP finally change how you play. Every specialist has a kit on [E] with alternates and upgrades, and each class gets two doctrines. Card schools have sets that change the rules, there are eight rule-breaker Legendaries, and gun levels branch. Guns take Modifications too. There’s also the Front Board, the opt-in Pact, cursed crates, PUSH ON and more. Recut 26 October with the roguelite locked down. There’s something new at every rank to 9, and cards can REPLACE and TRANSFORM. Field finds are on [L], the works are on [K], and the campaign runs over three fronts. Plus a codex, deployment codes, a chat filter and more.

Playtest 19

This one was all look and feel. The guns are rebuilt and move in your hands, soldiers have faces, and helmets fly off on a headshot. Explosions have weight, the weather changes while you watch, and there’s a newsreel of your best kills. We also took the slow motion out of Crack Shot and fixed the launch hiss and the glowing dots on walls. There’s more in the notes.

Playtest 18

Jerry gets named tank aces and a Fire Plan: the Doodlebug, a rolling barrage, Goliaths and the Paymaster. Every front has its own openers and set piece now. You get a crewed anti-tank gun, nine new Issue cards, your own men in co-op and a way out of Hold the Line. Press F10 to send us a report from the field. We checked 20,000 maps and fixed the crash and save-loss bugs. There’s more in the notes.

Playtest 17

The enemy fights like soldiers now and vaults the wire, with Stukas, Nebelwerfers and the Tiger behind them. There are counter-attack orders, daily conditions and a weekly operation, and you can Hold the Line past the relief. Every deployment is a fresh map. The M2 flamethrower is in, the whole battlefield looks and sounds new, and your squad is smarter.

Latest notes

How a deployment works

New

Pick a front at HQ, deploy, and hold the command post until the relief arrives.

Each wave

Hold the post

Enemies cross the field from the treeline. If the command post falls, or everyone is down, the deployment ends.

Between waves

Commendations

After each wave held you pick one of three commendation cards that boost you for the rest of the run. Some are tied to the gun you carry.

Wave 10 / 12 / 15

The relief

Each front has a relief wave. Hold off its heavier final assault and the front is secured. You get a citation, a bugle call and a relief bonus, doubled the first time.

The arsenal

New

Two guns at a time, chosen in the Armoury. Every gun has working iron sights.

Bolt-action

Kar98k

The headshot rifle. It’s slow and loud, and it hits hard.

Semi-auto

M1 Garand

Eight rounds and that ping.

Automatic

Thompson · B.A.R.

The Thompson is for close work. The B.A.R. holds a lane.

Close range

Trench Gun

For when they reach the sandbags.

Always

Grenades and bayonet

Hold G to cook a frag. V or middle mouse for a bayonet thrust.

On the ground

Enemy weapons

Fallen enemies can leave an MP40, StG 44, Panzerfaust or scoped Kar98k behind. Hold F to take it.

The enemy

New

Most of them are riflemen. The rest are there to break your rhythm.

  • Shock troopers and sappers. Shock troopers rush you. Sappers go for the wall. From wave 4, bayonet charges come in packs from one announced direction.
  • A Heavy Gunner comes every 5th wave. He has a burst-fire MG and a helmet that shrugs off the first headshot.
  • Flamethrower troopers and officers. Officers rally their men, so drop them first.
  • MG42 team. A gunner and loader dig in mid-field and sweep the wall.
  • An armoured car turns up on later waves, and a Panzer IV leads the final assault. Use the M1A1 bazooka on it.

Your post and your squad

New
F to man

M1919 nests

There are two sandbagged .30 cal nests on the ring. They overheat rather than run dry.

Hold B

Artillery

Kills fill a fire-mission meter. Raise the binoculars and click a target. An eight-shell barrage follows the red smoke.

Hired at HQ

Squadmates

You can hire up to three riflemen. They man the ring, move toward wherever the pressure is and shoot sappers first. Any you lose are replaced after each wave you hold.

Wave break

Quartermaster’s wagon

Spend this run’s requisition on grenades, a field dressing, sandbags, a fire mission or a borrowed gun.

Every run is different

New
  • Wave events start at wave 3: Creeping Barrage, Blitz, Sniper Nest, Supply Drop, Smoke Screen and Counter-Battery. Hold the wave for a bonus.
  • Standing orders. Once a front is secured you can opt into hardships like Alone, Ammunition Shortage or Hold At All Costs for more requisition.
  • Decorations. One-off medals for feats in a single deployment.
  • Specialists. Pick a role at HQ before you deploy: Rifleman, Machine Gunner, Medic, Engineer or Radioman.

Co-op

New
  • Host a game and share the join code, or join a friend’s. A section takes up to four soldiers.
  • Each player shops at the wagon with their own requisition. Commendations are picked in the wave break.
  • The host’s squad can deploy with you or stay home (there’s a toggle on the Platoon Roster).
  • A Field Manual explains things the first time they happen. The Options sheet covers audio, FOV, look and graphics.

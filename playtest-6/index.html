Latest notes

Commendation tiers

New

Cards now come as Basic, Rare, Epic or Legendary. Better odds as the waves climb, and a pity timer so you’re never starved.

  • 23 new action commendations that change how you play (Stripper Clips and friends), not just numbers.
  • Stronger tier colours and a Legendary gleam you can spot at a glance.

Rank, weapon levels and specialists

New
Rank 1–50

Player rank

XP from kills, waves and fronts. Ranks open weapons, fronts, specialists and squadmates. Insignia on the roll call and over your head. Field commission at 50.

Per gun

Weapon mastery

Each gun levels from the kills it makes. Levels open attachments in the new gunsmith: 55 of them over 7 slots, with plain pros and cons.

Per class

Specialist levels

Your specialist levels too, with a choice row at each step: smoke that blocks the line of fire, strafing runs, triage and more.

Command post and MGs

Changed
  • The command post is a room you can fall back into. Enemies on the roof are a threat; deal with them.
  • Squad crews free guns and you can shift the nests to prepared pits between waves.
  • The enemy’s main thrust is announced before it lands.
  • Mortar teams set up in sight of the wall, 60–75 m out, and are easier to find.

Accolades

New

Banners for impressive play, a feed of your teammates’ best moments and live section points. Recognition, not a leaderboard.

Difficulty retune

Changed
  • A gentler wave curve, with each front getting its own threat schedule.
  • An AT crate on the field before the Panzer arrives, shown on the HUD.
  • The bazooka carries five rockets instead of three.
  • Wave-held resupply loads your magazines a second after the hold.

HQ and menus

Changed
  • HQ is now a tabbed sheet that opens on a briefing, with a deploy slip and the lobby strip.
  • Menus hold 60 FPS (no more 100% GPU at HQ); new saves cap at 144.
  • A full text pass. Some names changed: Work Party, Battery on Call, Hot Barrel, Hand-Loaded Rounds, Hardened Troops, Steady Breath.

Fixes

Fixed
  • The B.A.R. attachment typo.
  • Long callsigns on the roll call shrink or cut short to stay in their column.
  • An error when an MG42 crew’s nest was cleared away.
  • Late waves no longer leave the MGs useless.

What we asked you to test

  • Does ranking up feel paced right, or too fast again?
  • The gunsmith: is it clear what each attachment does?
  • Can you get past wave 9 on the Farm now?

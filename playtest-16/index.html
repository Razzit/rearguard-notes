Cavalry

New enemy

Mounted troops now ride in on both factions’ fronts.

From wave 3–6, by front

Dismounting troops

A troop gallops in and gets down 42–50 m out, in cover if they can. They fight on foot with carbines and the horses bolt.

Later waves & an event

The sabre charge

You’ll hear a bugle and hooves first. Then a line of riders with sabres comes at the defenders, cuts as it passes, wheels round and comes again.

How to fight them

Horse or rider

Shoot the horse and the rider is thrown. Shoot the rider and the horse bolts. Either way you get a bonus. Kneel as a sabre passes and the blade may go over you.

Vehicle weak spots

New

Every vehicle has small orange-red glowing spots. Bullets there damage the hull, so you can kill a tank without a bazooka.

Panzer IV · Chi-Ha

Tanks

Driver’s slit ×2.5, engine grille and fuel drums ×2, turret ring and exhaust ×1.5.

Armoured car · Ha-Go

Light vehicles

Vision slit ×1.5, radiator grille ×1.25, tyres or sprockets ×1.

Half-track

Carriers

Radiator grille on the nose ×1.25, or shoot the gunner.

Roughly, it takes one Thompson about two magazines into a solo Panzer’s grille, and three rifles about 9 seconds. The bazooka is still much faster. A hit on a weak spot shows an orange hit marker with a metal clang. Anywhere else on the plate, it just clanks off. Steel-Core, Incendiary and Explosive Rounds add extra weak-spot damage.

Commendation cards

New
Key U

Reroll

Don’t like the offer? Swap all three cards for a fresh set. You hold one reroll. Once you’ve used it, it comes back after wave 3, 6, 9… It doesn’t work on Quartermaster’s Issue cards.

Hold Tab

What you’ve picked

Your commendations show up as small cards next to the roll call. You can see stacks, gun marks, a completed Legendary pair and your supply cards. Each teammate’s line has a short summary.

Fewer accidents

Deliberate picks

New cards take 0.6 s to become pickable (a brass line fills under them). A number key you were already pressing to switch weapons won’t pick one by accident.

New default keys

Changed

Weapon keys now follow what the weapon is, not which slot it landed in.

1 2 your guns3 trench shovel4 bazooka5 6 picked-up / extraF throw back a grenadeU reroll cards
  • Throwing back grenades is now on F, the use key. G always throws your own, so you’ll no longer cook your own grenade while trying to grab theirs.
  • If you’re on the old default keys you get the new ones automatically. Any keys you rebound yourself are kept.

In the field

Changed
  • Enemy weapon drops stay through the wave break. Grab them between waves. They’re cleared when the next wave starts.
  • Getting yourself up from last stand reloads every weapon, bazooka included. A revive from a teammate works the same as before.
  • The results screen adds up. There’s a new “Bounties, wave pay, teamwork” line, and the relief bonus says when it’s doubled for your first time securing that front.

HQ menus rebuilt

Changed

The words are plainer, the buttons are clearer and there’s less clutter.

  • A new BRIEFING page shows your kit at a glance, what to hold to win, and a NEXT UP list of the best thing to spend on.
  • Playing with friends is simpler. Press HOST A GAME, or paste a friend’s code and press JOIN. Your team of up to 6 is listed with a code you can copy.
  • Buttons say what they do (BUY, EQUIP, SELECT, HIRE), and each page has one solid main button.
  • UPGRADES › SQUAD is now one page for hiring, issue and training. Pages you haven’t unlocked stay hidden instead of greyed out.
  • No text is under 14 pt, so you can read the menus at 720p.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Long card text (Hot Potato, Shoulder Charge, the Lee-Enfield cards) no longer overlaps.
  • “Artillery ready” no longer sits on top of the weapon list, however many weapons you carry.
  • The black box with a pale circle drifting across the sky is gone.
  • Off-screen pings slide clear of the roster, compass and weapon list.
  • Picking up an enemy weapon no longer deletes your AT crate bazooka.
  • Drum Magazine isn’t offered when your Thompson already has the Type L Drum.
  • A switch-weapons key press can’t pick a card you didn’t want.
  • The results screen’s RP lines now add up to the total.
  • A full Field Manual scrolls instead of hiding every note’s text.
  • Joining by direct IP with a non-default port now connects.

What we’d like you to test

  • Cavalry: do the dismounting troops and the sabre charge feel fair? Can you read them coming?
  • Weak spots: can you see the glow at range, in fog and at night? Does killing a tank with bullets feel right, too easy or too hard?
  • Did the reroll and the Tab card list help you build what you wanted?
  • Do the new keys feel natural? Any clashes with your own bindings?
  • The new HQ menus: is anything harder to find than before?

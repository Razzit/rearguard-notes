The wagon is gone

Changed

No more running back to the quartermaster, and no shop menu. Supplies now come to you.

After waves 3, 6, 9…

Quartermaster’s Issue

Pick 1 of 3 supply cards. It comes right after that break’s commendation, on the same keys, and you can pick it mid-wave.

Mid-wave on 2, 5, 8…

Team supply crate

A crate parachutes in. Whoever opens it, the whole section gets what’s inside.

Before armour waves

AT crate

A bazooka for every soldier, or a rocket refill if you already have one. No racing for it.

Every deployment

All your RP banks

Nothing is bought mid-run, so every point of requisition goes home with you.

Supply cards: Field Dressing (+15 max HP, no heal), Hand-Loaded Rounds (+20% damage), Grenade Belt (+1 grenade and a refill), Fire Mission, Sandbag Detail and Replacement Riflemen.

A Legendary pair for every gun

New

Every gun has two Legendary commendations. Each is strong alone. Hold both and you complete a named pair with a bonus, announced to the whole section.

Once you hold one half, its partner turns up far more often.

Enemy weapons get perks too: Spoils of War makes pickups hit harder, reload faster and carry more rounds, and Captured Arsenal makes enemies drop weapons more often. Shovel, slide and walking soldiers

New
  • Trench shovel. Press 5 to draw it (you can rebind it). You move 15% faster with it out, and fire swings it. The quick bayonet on V still works.
  • Slide. Crouch while sprinting. You can steer a little, shoot, and jump out keeping your speed.
  • Walk cycles. Enemies, squadmates and other players now walk and run with their legs instead of gliding.

Berserker

Explosives and grenades

Changed
  • Booby Traps replace Tripwire Charges. This commendation plants charges on the enemy’s routes at the start of every wave. They never hurt your side, and every kill gets you a callout.
  • Throw grenades back. Grab a live enemy grenade near you with the grenade key, if you’re quick. The Hot Potato commendation makes it easy: longer reach, instant grab and a harder throw.
  • Enemy grenades are much less accurate early on and tighten up as the waves go on.

Battalion HQ and co-op

New
  • Pioneer Platoon is a new Battalion facility that digs extra MG pits on every front. The host’s level decides.
  • Enemy strength readout. Waves always grew with the number of soldiers. Now each co-op wave tells you by how much, for example “Enemy strength for 6 soldiers: +275% men, +50% tougher”.
  • Faster revives. The more of you revive someone, the faster it goes:
2 soldiers ×1.53 soldiers ×24 or more ×2.5

Fixes

Fixed
  • Enemies no longer spawn inside bushes, hedges or cover.
  • Enemies on the first map no longer get stuck on the tank or in the cottage.
  • Bodies in tight rooms settle instead of jittering and flying about.
  • The host’s name no longer resets to “Host” after a round.
  • Rejoin the same deployment and you keep your commendations and supply cards.
  • Gunfire no longer keeps playing on the results page.
  • The bazooka reloads when you switch back to it.
  • Magazine upgrades fill your gun to the new size straight away.
  • Scopes keep the game’s colour grade.
  • Field Manual text no longer overlaps the close button.
  • At most three enemy health bars show, kept out of the centre of the screen.

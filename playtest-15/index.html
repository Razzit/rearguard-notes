Rearguard · Field dispatch to all testers

Playtest 15 Patch Notes

Thanks for every note from playtest 14. This build acts on almost all of it: the wagon is gone, every gun has a Legendary pair to chase, there's a shovel, a slide, and a lot of fixes.

Build: playtest 15 test buildCo-op: 1–6 soldiersSaves: carried over automatically

The wagon is gone

Changed

No more running back to the quartermaster each break, and no shop menu. Supplies now come to you.

Quartermaster's Issue

After waves 3, 6, 9 and so on, each soldier picks 1 of 3 supply cards. It appears right after that break's commendation and uses the same keys. You can pick it mid-wave.

Team supply crates

A crate parachutes in mid-wave on waves 2, 5, 8 and so on. Whoever opens it, the whole section gets what's inside.

AT crate

Before every armour wave a crate drops with a bazooka for each soldier, or a rocket refill if you already have one. No racing for it.

All your RP banks

Nothing is bought during a deployment, so every point of requisition goes home with you for gun levels, HQ and fronts.

Supply cards: Field Dressing (+15 max HP, no heal), Hand-Loaded Rounds (+20% damage), Grenade Belt (+1 grenade and a refill), Fire Mission, Sandbag Detail and Replacement Riflemen.

Legendary pairs for every gun

New

Every gun now has two Legendary commendations. Each is strong on its own. Hold both and you complete a named pair with an extra effect, announced to the whole section. Once you hold one half, its partner turns up far more often.

Ammo mutators work on any gun you fire, including pickups: Incendiary Rounds and Steel-Core Rounds (Rare), Shock Rounds (Epic) and Explosive Rounds (Legendary). Tracer colour shows your ammo. Explosive Rounds takes one of your two Legendary slots, so choose: the gun pair or the universal boom.

Enemy weapons: Spoils of War (Rare) makes picked-up enemy weapons hit harder, reload faster and come with more rounds. Captured Arsenal (Epic) makes enemies drop weapons more often. None of these blasts hurt teammates.

Armoury: gun levels and two new guns

New
  • Gun levels. Spend RP at the Armoury to raise a gun from level 1 to 10. Each level gives +5% damage and +3% reload speed. Cheaper guns level up cheaper.
  • Capped by Battle Honours. The level that counts in a run is capped by the Honours tier you're playing, from level 4 on Standard up to 10 on Honours X. The HUD shows when your gun is capped.
  • M1911A1 pistol is the new free starter. Level it up, then move on to a rifle. The Kar98k now costs 400 RP to unlock. If you already have the Kar98k, you keep it.
  • Lee-Enfield No. 4. A fast-bolt .303 rifle with 10 rounds. Unlocks at rank 6 for 1,600 RP.

Shovel, slide and walking soldiers

New
  • Trench shovel. Press 5 (rebindable) to draw it. You move 15% faster with it out. Fire swings it. The quick bayonet on V / middle mouse still works.
  • Melee commendations: Lunge (dash onto a man up to 7 m away), Bloodlust (melee kills heal), Cleave (hit everyone in a wide arc), Ground Pound (swing in mid-air to slam) and Berserker (melee kills stack speed).
  • Slide. Crouch while sprinting to slide. You can steer a little, shoot, and jump out of it keeping your speed.
  • Walk cycles. Enemies, squadmates and other players now walk and run with their legs instead of gliding.

Explosives and grenades

Changed
  • Booby Traps replace Tripwire Charges. This Rare commendation plants charges on the enemy's approach routes at the start of every wave. They never hurt your side, and you get credit and a callout for every kill.
  • Throw grenades back. Anyone can grab a live enemy grenade close by with the grenade key, if you're quick. The Hot Potato commendation makes it easy: longer reach, instant grab, harder throw.
  • Enemy grenades are much less accurate in early waves and tighten up as the waves go on.

Battalion HQ and co-op

New
  • Pioneer Platoon, a new Battalion facility, digs extra MG pits on every front. The host's level decides.
  • Enemy strength readout. Waves have always grown with the number of soldiers. Now each co-op wave tells you by how much, e.g. "Enemy strength for 6 soldiers: +275% men, +50% tougher".
  • Faster revives. More hands revive faster: ×1.5 for two, ×2 for three, ×2.5 for four or more.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Enemies no longer spawn inside bushes, hedges or other cover (0 in 2,800 test spawns).
  • Enemies on the first map no longer get stuck on the tank or in the burnt cottage.
  • Bodies in tight rooms like the command post settle down instead of jittering and flying about.
  • The host's name no longer resets to "Host" after a round.
  • If you disconnect and rejoin the same deployment, you keep your commendations and supply cards.
  • Gunfire no longer keeps playing on the results page.
  • The bazooka reloads when you switch back to it, for example after calling artillery.
  • Magazine upgrades fill your gun to the new size straight away.
  • Scopes keep the game's colour grade instead of losing it.
  • Field Manual text no longer overlaps the close button.
  • Enemy health bars are capped at three and kept out of the centre of the screen.

  • Is a supply card every 3rd wave the right pace? Did you miss buying things from the wagon?
  • How do the shovel, slide and melee commendations feel?
  • Do gun level prices feel fair for the RP you're banking?
  • Any enemy still in a bush, stuck on scenery, or a wrong name after a round? Tell us the map and wave.
  • Scope users: does the view look right now?

The DEPLOY page

New

HQ now opens on DEPLOY: your section, a READY FOR YOU card with what’s worth doing next, and a deploy bar linked to the front you picked.

  • Picking a front from CHANGE FRONT closes the sheet, and DEPLOY works with it open.

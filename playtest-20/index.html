Playtest 20
Patch Notes

PLAYTEST
20

You told us the gunplay feels clean, but your class was just a bigger number and every upgrade was more flat damage. This build goes after that. Every specialist now carries their own kit and picks a doctrine. RP buys new cards, kits and run boons instead of +10% damage. Cards belong to schools, and collecting a set changes the rules. You pick your run on the Front Board, raise the stakes with the Pact if you want, and make choices at forks along the way. The recut adds the roguelite lock-down on top. There’s something new at every rank. Some cards replace others, and some transform how a thing works. The men you drop can leave finds, and your pioneers build works in the breaks. There’s a three-front campaign, and the codex keeps what you learn whether you win or lose.

Recut 26 October. This build went out twice. The first cut had the class kits, doctrines, gun branches and the first roguelite round. The recut adds the lock-down round, and the sections marked Recut below are new. The Front Board, slips and the Pact also open sooner now. It’s still version 0.20.0, so grab the new zip from the Discord. You’ll get the What’s New card again at HQ.

Every class carries kit

New

Every specialist now has a piece of kit on E during a wave. Its charges show over your weapons and as pips on the section roster. You get them back each wave you hold. Pick yours under LOADOUT › SPECIALIST.

  • Rifleman: Running Tally. Each headshot kill in a row puts a notch on your rifle. In order, the notches give you a faster bolt, no sway after a sprint, an instant reload, a round that goes through two men, and a headshot that hits a heavy twice as hard. At five notches, press E to call STEADY, LADS!, which steadies the whole section’s aim for three seconds. A body-shot kill or taking a hit starts the count again. Also, every rifleman’s headshot now goes straight through a helmet.
  • Machine Gunner: Ammunition Box. Set a box down and anyone can hold F at it for full magazines and a grenade. Everyone gets their own share, so there’s no racing for it.
  • Medic: Syrettes. Jab the man in reach. He gets health back, and for a few seconds hits don’t knock his aim. On a downed mate it holds his bleeding for 20 seconds, so you can finish the fight first.
  • Engineer: Sandbag Pack. Fill a blown gap in the sandbags at once, or throw up a short wall wherever you’re looking, mid-fight. Your mates can hop it. The enemy has to go round.
  • Radioman: Signal Flares. Fire a flare where you’re looking. Every man under it is marked and takes more damage from the whole section. At night it lights the ground as well.
  • Alternate kits open from class level 6. You buy each one once with RP, carry one at a time, and swap free at HQ. The rifleman gets Sniper’s Veil, Dummy Head and Marksman’s Challenge. The gunner gets Tripod Mount and Captured Arms. The medic has Aid Post and Rescue Smoke, the engineer Wire Party and Bangalore Torpedo, and the radioman Supply Drop Call and SOS Targets. Every kit has upgrades that change how it works.

Doctrines: two ways to play each class

New

Pick one on the SPECIALIST page. Swapping is free at HQ. If you don’t pick one, your class plays as it did before.

  • Rifleman: Deliberate Fire (a headshot kill makes your next shot dead true) or Skirmisher (every kill gives you a burst of speed and true hip fire).
  • Machine Gunner: Beaten Zone (a held burst pins every man it hits) or Marching Fire (fire on the move, and kills put rounds back).
  • Medic: Field Surgeon (hold F on a man up to 15 m off to patch him or get him up) or Combat Medic (your kills heal you and everyone near you).
  • Engineer: Assault Pioneer (a grenade close to armour hits like a bazooka rocket) or Bulwark (crouch still to dig in).
  • Radioman: Fire Director (spotted men charge your fire missions when anyone kills them) or Signaller (a free precision shell every 45 seconds).

What your RP buys now

Changed
  • No more +10% damage. The Quartermaster’s Tier I lines are gone, and every RP you spent on them is refunded. Instead, your soldier toughens up with service. Service Conditioning I–VI comes free at ranks 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30. By rank 30 you’re exactly as strong as a fully upgraded soldier was. Battle Honours pay a little more too.
  • The Commendation Dossier (rank 4). New soldiers start with a plainer deck, though since the recut it has twelve more Rare and Epic cards in it. At UPGRADES › SOLDIER you requisition new Rare and Epic cards into your deck, picking from three on offer. Veterans already own the lot.
  • Blueprints. Now and then an officer carries a blueprint for a card you don’t have. Pick it up where he falls and bring it home at the relief to keep it. If you die or get overrun, it’s lost.
  • Return to stores. Take up to four cards out of your own deck to aim a build. Free, and you can put them back.
  • Requisition Slips (rank 3 since the recut, and the first one is free). You take one boon into each deployment, chosen at BRIEFING. The choices include a lucky reroll, an early Issue, a Dressing Station, Corporals for your riflemen, a gun zeroed one level higher and a Legendary half on your first offer.

Cards that change the rules

New
  • Schools and sets. Every commendation belongs to a school: Marksman, Suppression, Close Quarters, Demolition, Fieldcraft, Signals or Medical. Hold 3 or 5 cards of one school and a new rule comes in, like One Round Back for marksmen. A card that would complete a set says so before you pick it (“» MARKSMAN 2/3”).
  • Rule-breakers. There are eight new Legendaries that bend how your other cards work. Since the recut they get a slot of their own, one per deployment, and can turn up from rank 3.
  • Rank II. A second copy of some action cards comes as RANK II. You get a new trick, not a bigger number.
  • Class cards (from class level 2). Each class has two commendations nobody else can draw, plus a class card in the Quartermaster’s Issue.
  • Your class’s favourite cards are now its action cards rather than its number cards. The strongest number cards also stack less.

Picks that matter

Recut
  • Either/or. Some cards can’t be held together. A red line says what a card rules out or REPLACES. If you take it, you hand the old one back.
  • Seven Epic transforms, one for each school: Drawn Bead, Traversing Fire, Put the Boot In, Fire Bombs, Shell Scrape, Strafing Run and Blood Up. Each one changes how a thing works, not how big it is. They’re dossier cards, so you requisition them.
  • The pair comes. Hold one half of your gun’s Legendary pair and its partner turns up within two offers. A Legendary from a cursed crate goes in a third Legendary slot.
  • Something that grows. After the first offer in a deployment, every offer includes a card you already have that can go up, next to the new ones. The first offer is just the draw as it falls.

The roguelite starts sooner

Recut
  • Each promotion brings one new system. Rank 2 opens the Front Board, rank 3 requisition slips, rank 4 the dossier and rank 5 Service Conditioning. Then rank 6 dispatches, rank 7 the board’s third offer, rank 8 the works and rank 9 the campaign. If you rank up slowly, you still get the board, slips, dossier and dispatches after one, two, three and four deployments, whichever comes first. Run conditions start from your second deployment.
  • RULES IN FORCE: Brass tags under the wave count show what your build is doing: sets, rule-breaker, pair, burden, Jerry’s doctrine, the section’s rules, a dare, your finds, the works, the campaign stop. Hold Tab to see your mates’.
  • NEXT BREAK under the wave banner tells you what the coming break brings: a commendation, the Quartermaster’s Issue, a cursed crate, a push-on vote, the works, a dare, a route vote, the march.
  • SAME ORDERS: One press on the after-action page puts you back out with the same front, board, Pact and kit on a fresh map.

Pick your run

New
  • The Front Board (rank 2 since the recut, with the third offer at rank 7). At BRIEFING each front offers three runs: the front as it comes, one with a Warning and an Anomaly, or one with two Warnings. Warnings make it harder (night, fog, elites, an armoured column, a busy front). Anomalies are odd gifts. The RP and XP bonus is printed before you pick. In co-op the host picks. Since the recut, Anomalies cost a little pay and a run’s pay tops out at x3 before Battle Honours. Each Warning also names the school that answers it, and that school’s cards come three times as often while the Warning holds.
  • The Pact (from Battle Honours I since the recut). You sign lines for heat, like flamethrowers every wave, no Issue after wave 3 or tanks two waves early. Each point of heat pays more RP and XP, and beating your best heat on a front pays a bounty. Heat 0 is the game as it was. The Pact clears after each deployment unless you tick KEEP.
  • Daily Orders and six Challenges went on the board in the recut, with their pay printed. A friend’s BRIEFING shows the orders the host picked.

Forks in the run

New
  • Cursed crate. Sometimes a break offers you a crate of your own. It holds a strong card with a printed burden for the next two waves. Only you carry it, and you never have to take it.
  • Perfect wave. Some waves set a target, like keeping the post untouched. Meet it and the next card comes a tier higher.
  • PUSH ON. After some waves, hold the ready key to vote to skip the break for a reward. It passes with more than half the section. A quick tap still just readies you.
  • Route. On Hold the Line you vote between Armour Push, Night Attack and Shock Troops, and each one pays more. Since the recut, single fronts also choose a route for waves 7 to 9 in the wave-6 Issue break. G-2 names the ace you’ll meet at the final assault.

Field finds

Recut
  • Officers, snipers, gunners and elites you drop may leave you something. It’s lit gold and only you can see it. It might be a captured gun with a printed trait, or a gun part that raises your gun to TIER II and then III (brass bands on the barrel). Or it’s field kit on its own key, L: a Smoke Pot, a Flare Pistol that marks men, or an MG Belt.
  • Hold F to take a find. In a wave, J gives the find you’re looking at to the nearest mate.

The works

Recut
  • From rank 8, holding waves earns the section PIONEERS. Each break you can spend them on one piece of works, and it stands for the rest of the deployment. You pick from a spare MG pit, wire, a breastwork out front, a raised parapet or a listening post. It’s sited facing where the next attack comes from. Press K to vote, or KEEP THE PIONEERS.
  • The works can share a break with one other vote (push on, a dare, a route, the lorries). K votes on the works and the ready key votes on the other one. Only the campaign’s march stands the works down, and the pioneers march with you.
  • LANE INTEL: Every break tells you how many are coming, from which side and what they’re bringing. There’s a wedge on the compass too.

The campaign

Recut
  • From rank 9 there’s THE CAMPAIGN on the Front Board. It’s three fronts in one run, five waves a stop. The route map forks after the first and second stops. Every stop prints its curse, its pay and the school that answers it, and the finale names its ace. Later stops fight like the middle of a deployment rather than the end.
  • Your cards, Issue cards and kit go with you on the march. The works and anything left lying around stay behind. Hold a stop and your requisition is banked there and then.
  • The section votes on each fork with the ready key. Most votes wins, and a tie goes to the host. Every campaign has its own short code, and SAME GROUND? replays the whole route.

The Pact opens doors

Recut
  • Heat 2: Jerry plays doctrines (snipers in the trees, flamethrower teams, officers leading). The break tells you which, and how to beat it. Heat 4: the front’s own event. Heat 6: the Captured Orders Issue card. Heat 9: the ace’s Kampfgruppe in the final assault. Heat 12: Jerry’s best.
  • Each front has its own event and Issue card. The events are Hedgerow Infiltration, Ski Troops, Mule Train, House to House and Night Infiltration. The Issue cards are Farmer’s Guide, Winter Rum, Mule-Borne Ammunition, Falling Masonry and Coastwatcher’s Map. You can also get them from Battle Honours IV.
  • The Veteran Dossier: 14 new commendations, earned as you secure fronts at Battle Honours II, IV and VI.
  • RECORDS shows each front’s heat ladder and a 100-cell completion grid. Every cell pays once.

Every loss counts

Recut
  • The after-action says what broke the line, how close you came and what’s next. Anything new is kept: enemy files, set rules you found, pairs you completed and whatever overran you.
  • RECORDS › CODEX lists every commendation, Issue card and threat you’ve met, and the ones still to find. It spells out pairs, set rules and the draw odds.
  • Every deployment has a code. Copy it to share a run, or press SAME GROUND? to fight it again. A campaign’s route and the day’s orders come back with it.
  • The Sar’nt-Major has a word about your last deployment on the BRIEFING page. The Battalion’s story moves on every time you go out.

Guns and nests

New
  • Gun levels branch. At gun levels 3, 6 and 9 every gun offers a choice between two ways to fight. You can swap free at the Armoury. From level 4 each gun takes a Modification, bought once with RP. Anything a mod costs you is printed in red.
  • The M1911 gets a Legendary pair: Stopping Power + Hammer Pair = Gunfighter. It’s also steadier, with a tighter spread, less kick and less sway on the sights.
  • Nest Armament (rank 18). Your nests can take AP belts that go through one man into the next, or an M2 .50 cal that pins men and punches through light cover. A Traversing Stop stakes out each gun’s killing ground. The M1919 Gun Drill is retired and refunded.
  • Two new camps (rank 18). Forward Pits puts the guns out ahead of the wire. The Inner Redoubt rings the post with a low wall. The host picks the defences at BRIEFING.
  • Automatic tagging. Hit a heavy, officer, crewman, sniper or vehicle and the section sees him tagged for a few seconds. Your men call it out too. Marked men show through smoke, fog and night.

Fight together

New
  • Your engineer and medic now help you, not only the AI squad. Six of the same class still count as one, so nothing stacks silly.
  • Section Drills. Two soldiers of different classes fighting close together form a drill with a small bonus for both. A gold diamond on the roster shows who’s in one.
  • Hand it on. Press J in a wave break to give a mate a card you didn’t pick. It turns up in his next offer.
  • The roster and the roll call show everyone’s class, kit, slip and best cards at a glance.

Section play

Recut · co-op
  • SECTION SETS: When enough of you each hold the same school’s set, a section rule comes in for everyone.
  • Your cards lean toward what your mates are building, and they say so: “MARKSMAN WITH BAKER”, “FUSES WITH BAKER”.
  • AMMUNITION FUSION: When two of you hit the same man with different special rounds, you get FLASHOVER, PANIC, EARTHED and more.
  • THE SECTION DARE: Every few breaks there’s a dare for the next wave, with its pay and its cost printed. Hold ready to take it.
  • The host decides every find and every vote, so all six machines agree. If you drop and rejoin, you get your finds, tier and kit back.

Keys

Changed
  • E in a wave uses your kit while it has charges.
  • Crouch and press E for your class’s once-a-wave AT mine, clearing post or sandbag wall.
  • E at the post between waves is still READY. Hold it during a PUSH ON break to vote to push on, or to take a dare.
  • J hands a card on in a break and gives a find in a wave. K votes the works. L uses your field kit. Hold F at an Ammunition Box to draw from it, or at a find to take it. They’re all rebindable at OPTIONS › CONTROLS.

Your save keeps everything

Changed
  • Nothing you bought is lost. Tier I and the Gun Drill are refunded in full, and the BRIEFING says how much.
  • Your classes get their starter kits on first load. A class already at level 9 gets its first new kit free. If you owned the Spotter’s Scope, you now carry the Running Tally, and its upgrade came across with it.
  • Everyone in a co-op game needs the playtest 20 recut. Anyone on an older build gets a plain message to update instead of a silent drop.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Chat filter: racial slurs are blotted out of chat lines and callsigns. Swearing is left alone.
  • Field glasses now say GUNS SILENT during Counter-Battery instead of a full NOT READY meter (field report 3).
  • A six-card commendation or Issue strip now narrows to stay clear of the screen edges and the HUD corners (field report 7).
  • The Tab sheet and the commendation sheet fit at 720p and 130% HUD size with six men. The BRIEFING’s veteran column fits at 1080p and 720p.
  • PUSH ON: Esc doesn’t decline it, and a ready section doesn’t wait on it.
  • Six players: heals from others, damage cuts that aren’t from your own kit, and team effects are now capped. A full section can’t stack itself invulnerable. Your own heals are never capped.

Known issues

  • Kits only work during a wave, because E between waves is READY.
  • Modifications don’t show on the gun model yet.
  • Some numbers are set for this playtest only and may change after it. That includes the Pact’s pay, how easily the Running Tally breaks, and the recut’s card odds and works prices.
  • Nobody played the recut’s changes before it went out. They were scored by machines against a roguelite rubric.

What we’d like you to test

  • Which kit and doctrine did you take, and did they change how you played your class?
  • Did the Front Board, the Pact and the forks feel like real choices, or did you always pick the same thing?
  • Did you chase a school set? Did a rule-breaker change your run?
  • Rifleman: does the Running Tally feel good, or does it break too easily?
  • Recut: does a new system at every rank teach itself, or is it too much? Do REPLACE and TRANSFORM cards feel like decisions? Did your gun’s pair turn up within two offers?
  • Recut: how much heat did you sign, and were the doors it opened worth it? Did the campaign’s fork votes go smoothly in a section?
  • Recut: did L and K come to hand, or did you forget the finds and the works were there?
  • Did E, crouch + E, F or the push-on hold ever do the wrong thing?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

You told us the gunplay feels clean but your class was just a bigger number, and every upgrade was more flat damage. This one changes that. Every specialist now carries kit of their own and picks a doctrine. Your RP buys new cards, kits and run boons instead of +10% damage. Cards come in schools that change the rules when you collect them, and every run is a choice: pick it on the Front Board, raise the stakes with the Pact, and take the forks along the way.

Every class carries kit

New

Each specialist has a piece of kit on E during a wave. Its charges show over your weapons and as pips on the section roster, and they come back each wave you hold. Pick yours under LOADOUT › SPECIALIST.

  • Rifleman: Running Tally. Headshot kills in a row notch your rifle up: a faster bolt, no sway after a sprint, an instant reload, a round that goes through two men, then a headshot that hits a heavy twice as hard. At five notches, E calls STEADY, LADS! and steadies the whole section’s aim for three seconds. A body-shot kill or taking a hit starts the count again. Every rifleman’s headshot now goes straight through a helmet.
  • Machine Gunner: Ammunition Box. Set a box down; any soldier holds F at it for full magazines and a grenade. Everyone gets their own share, so nobody races for it.
  • Medic: Syrettes. A jab for the man in reach: health and a few seconds where hits don’t knock his aim, or, on a downed mate, it holds his bleeding for 20 seconds so you can finish the fight first.
  • Engineer: Sandbag Pack. Fill a blown gap in the sandbags at once, or throw up a short wall where you look, mid-fight. Your mates can hop it; the enemy has to go round.
  • Radioman: Signal Flares. Fire a flare where you look: every man under it is marked and takes more damage from the whole section, and at night it lights the ground too.
  • Alternate kits open from class level 6 and are bought once with RP; you carry one and swap free at HQ. Sniper’s Veil, Dummy Head and Marksman’s Challenge for the rifleman; Tripod Mount and Captured Arms for the gunner; Aid Post and Rescue Smoke for the medic; Wire Party and Bangalore Torpedo for the engineer; Supply Drop Call and SOS Targets for the radioman. Every kit has upgrades that change how it works.

Doctrines: two ways to play each class

New

Pick one on the SPECIALIST page and swap it free at HQ. With none picked, your class plays as before.

  • Rifleman: Deliberate Fire (a headshot kill makes your next shot dead true) or Skirmisher (every kill gives you a burst of speed and true hip fire).
  • Machine Gunner: Beaten Zone (a held burst pins every man it hits) or Marching Fire (fire on the move, and kills put rounds back).
  • Medic: Field Surgeon (hold F on a man up to 15 m off to patch him or get him up) or Combat Medic (your kills heal you and everyone near you).
  • Engineer: Assault Pioneer (a grenade close to armour hits like a bazooka rocket) or Bulwark (crouch still to dig in).
  • Radioman: Fire Director (spotted men charge your fire missions when anyone kills them) or Signaller (a free precision shell every 45 seconds).

What your RP buys now

Changed
  • No more +10% damage. The Quartermaster’s Tier I lines are gone, and every RP you spent on them is refunded. Your soldier now hardens with service instead: Service Conditioning I–VI comes free at ranks 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30. By rank 30 you are exactly as strong as a fully upgraded soldier was. Battle Honours pay a little more.
  • The Commendation Dossier (rank 4). New soldiers start with a plainer deck. At UPGRADES › SOLDIER, requisition new Rare and Epic cards into your deck from three on offer. Veterans already own the lot.
  • Blueprints. Now and then an officer carries a blueprint for a card you don’t have. Pick it up where he falls and bring it home at the relief to keep it; die or get overrun and it’s lost.
  • Return to stores. Take up to four cards out of your own deck to aim a build. Free, and you can put them back.
  • Requisition Slips (rank 9). Carry one boon into each deployment, chosen at BRIEFING: a lucky reroll, an early Issue, a Dressing Station, Corporals for your riflemen, a gun zeroed one level higher, a Legendary half on your first offer, and more.

Cards that change the rules

New
  • Schools and sets. Every commendation belongs to a school: Marksman, Suppression, Close Quarters, Demolition, Fieldcraft, Signals or Medical. Hold 3 or 5 cards of one school and a rule comes in, such as One Round Back for marksmen. A card that would complete a set says so before you pick it.
  • Rule-breakers. Eight new Legendaries that bend how your other cards work. Your first finished set puts one in your next offer.
  • Rank II. A second copy of some action cards comes as RANK II, with a new trick instead of a bigger number.
  • Class cards (from class level 2): two commendations each class can draw that nobody else can, plus a class card in the Quartermaster’s Issue.
  • Your class’s favourite cards are now its action cards, not its number cards, and the strongest number cards stack less.

Pick your run

New
  • The Front Board (rank 7). At BRIEFING each front offers three runs: the front as it comes, one with a Warning and an Anomaly, or one with two Warnings. Warnings make it harder (night, fog, elites, an armoured column, a busy front) and Anomalies are odd gifts; the RP and XP bonus is printed before you pick. In co-op the host picks.
  • The Pact (once you’ve secured a front at Battle Honours III). Sign lines for heat: flamethrowers every wave, no Issue after wave 3, tanks two waves early, and more. Each point of heat pays more RP and XP, and beating your best heat on a front pays a bounty. Heat 0 is the game as before.

Forks in the run

New
  • Cursed crate. Sometimes a break offers your own crate: a strong card with a printed burden for the next two waves. Only you carry it, and you never have to take it.
  • Perfect wave. Some waves set a target, like keeping the post untouched. Meet it and the next card comes a tier higher.
  • PUSH ON. After some waves, hold the ready key to vote to skip the break for a reward. More than half the section carries it; a quick tap still just readies you.
  • Route. On Hold the Line, vote between Armour Push, Night Attack and Shock Troops, each paying more.

Guns and nests

New
  • Gun levels branch. At gun levels 3, 6 and 9 every gun offers a choice between two ways to fight, free to swap at the Armoury. From level 4 each gun takes a Modification bought once with RP; anything a mod costs you is printed in red.
  • The M1911 gets a Legendary pair: Stopping Power + Hammer Pair = Gunfighter. It is also steadier: a tighter spread, less kick and less sway on the sights.
  • Nest Armament (rank 18): AP belts that go through a man into the next, or an M2 .50 cal that pins men and punches through light cover. A Traversing Stop stakes out each gun’s killing ground. The M1919 Gun Drill is retired and refunded.
  • Two new camps (rank 18): Forward Pits puts the guns out ahead of the wire; the Inner Redoubt rings the post with a low wall. The host picks the defences at BRIEFING.
  • Automatic tagging. Hit a heavy, officer, crewman, sniper or vehicle and the section sees him tagged for a few seconds, with a shout from your men. Marked men show through smoke, fog and night.

Fight together

New
  • Your engineer and medic now help you, not only the AI squad. Six of the same class still count as one, so nothing stacks silly.
  • Section Drills. Two soldiers of different classes fighting close together form a drill with a small bonus for both. A gold diamond on the roster shows who’s in one.
  • Hand it on. Press J in a wave break to give a mate a card you didn’t pick; it turns up in his next offer.
  • The roster and the roll call show everyone’s class, kit, slip and best cards at a glance.

Keys

Changed
  • E in a wave uses your kit while it has charges.
  • Crouch and press E for your class’s once-a-wave AT mine, clearing post or sandbag wall.
  • E at the post between waves is still READY; hold it during a PUSH ON break to vote to push on.
  • J hands a card on. Hold F at an Ammunition Box to draw from it.

Your save keeps everything

Changed
  • Nothing you bought is lost. Tier I and the Gun Drill are refunded in full, and the BRIEFING says how much.
  • Your classes draw their starter kits on first load, and a class already at level 9 gets its first new kit free. If you owned the Spotter’s Scope you now carry the Running Tally, and its upgrade carried over.
  • Everyone in a co-op game needs the playtest 20 build.

Known issues

  • Kits only work during a wave, because E between waves is READY.
  • Modifications don’t show on the gun model yet.
  • Some numbers are set for this playtest only and may move after it, including the Pact’s pay and how easily the Running Tally breaks.

What we’d like you to test

  • Did you miss the Tier I lines? Did your RP feel better spent on cards, kits and slips?
  • Which kit and doctrine did you take, and did they change how you played your class?
  • Did the Front Board, the Pact and the forks feel like real choices, or did you always pick the same thing?
  • Did you chase a school set? Did a rule-breaker change your run?
  • Rifleman: does the Running Tally feel good, or does it break too easily?
  • Did E, crouch + E, F or the push-on hold ever do the wrong thing?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong; it sends the map code for you.

Playtest 33
Patch Notes

PLAYTEST
33

On Sunday afternoon Lachie wrote five posts on the HQ: you buy everything you can afford, set it, and never come back. He was right, and the code said why. A commissioned soldier has every rank gate open, the whole catalogue costs about a million RP, and a maxed run banks fifty to sixty thousand a deployment, so it is all bought in fifteen to twenty-five deployments and nothing asks for a choice after that. This build is that rework, measured against the testers’ own usage lines, built as hide-not-delete so every save loads and every RP comes back. Plus the final wave at a sane pace, and the music fix.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is. On your first visit to the HQ you will see up to four lines returning RP, your worn perks trimmed to your rank’s slots, and a smaller grenade pouch until your rank catches up. Nothing is lost: it is all still owned.

One PERKS list

New

The tier 2 and 3 trees and the Platoon lines were a catalogue you emptied. They are one list now, and what matters is what you wear.

  • UPGRADES > SOLDIER has one PERKS slot and one list. What you wear comes first, then FIREPOWER, FIELDCRAFT, LOGISTICS and VETERAN. Each row has one button: BUY, WEAR or TAKE OFF. Prices are unchanged.
  • Slots come with rank and nothing else. None before 13, one at 13, two at 19, three at 30. A commissioned soldier has three. A veteran wearing six keeps the first three and is told once; the rest stay owned.
  • Veteran Traits are rows in the same list and wear in the same slots. Veteran Points buy traits only. The Battalion HQ’s TRAITS page uses the same slots and says SLOTS FULL.
  • SECTION CONDITIONING replaces the squad’s three training lines. Each Service Conditioning step gives your men +15% health and +10% aim, and step III adds Corporals. In co-op the host’s rank counts. The 28,000 RP comes back once. In our soaks the men’s share of kills did not rise; they stopped dying.
  • The WEAPONS tick box reads WITH YOUR RANK AND PERKS and says on hover where the extra comes from. The old Tier 1 lines were retired in Playtest 26; the box’s name made them look alive.

STORES

New

The mid-game RP sink that is a choice a run, not a bar you fill.

  • STORES is the Battalion HQ’s first view from rank 26. The host picks up to three caches for the section’s next deployment and pays. Clients propose. UNPICK before deploying gives the RP back. The caches are used up when the deployment ends, win or lose.
  • The six caches, at Lachie’s prices: AT cache, 25k, a bazooka on every soldier’s rack. Weapons cache, 25k, a captured gun on every rack. Flamethrower cache, 25k. Ammunition cache, 10k, a box by the post with two draws a soldier. Repair stores, 35k, a box in the post that puts 30% back once. Fire plan, 50k, every fire mission ready at wave 1.
  • The BRIEFING’s base card lists what is picked, the same on every machine.
  • The emergency flare barrage was not built. It would have flattened the early fight we had just measured as too easy. The fire plan is its nearest version.

Stood down, RP returned

Changed

Nothing is deleted. Each of these is hidden behind a switch, stays in your save, and pays back what you spent, once, with a line on its page.

  • The Battalion HQ’s six facilities. Fourteen of their eighteen levels were flat percentage bumps and the Pioneers’ pits answered a problem the arsenal solved. Up to 204,000 RP back. The FUND and TRAITS stay.
  • Six requisition slips: Lucky Charm, Advance Party, Dressing Station, Corporals, Field Allowance and Zeroed Sights, which had been dead since Playtest 27 yet was still for sale. 14,500 RP back for all six. Spare Pouch, Armourer’s Note and Old Hand stay, and Old Hand is renamed HEAD START so it no longer shares a Veteran trait’s name.
  • The Grenadier upgrade. The pouch grows with rank instead: 2 grenades, 3 at rank 10, 4 at 20, 5 at 30. Cards and the Spare Pouch still add more. 2,265 RP back.
  • The mastery 3, 6 and 9 gun picks. Half were re-hashes of the settled shot and the pin, and the A side was silently in force for everyone who never opened the panel. Nothing is in force now; your picks stay saved. Attachments carry a gun’s variation.
  • Your gun’s Modification is the gunsmith’s eighth slot, beside the sights, barrel and muzzle, outside the fitted-part cap. FIELD SHEET’s figures now include it.

HARDER

Changed
  • HARDER is one list: the ten handicaps and the eleven Pact lines with one figure for what they pay. The figure is the two old ones multiplied, which is what the reward actually uses.
  • Three presets: THE HARD WAY, NO QUARTER and THE LONG NIGHT. One click signs the set, a second strikes it out, no fee. Relieving a front with a set signed wins its clasp.
  • KEEP SIGNED is on by default once you have held Honours III. The Pact’s heat always worked; nobody met it because lines were struck out after every deployment.
  • Chits re-roll the cards at most three times a deployment, per player. The strip shows what is left.
  • A friend’s THE HOST’S BASE card on the BRIEFING now opens UPGRADES > BASE, as the host’s does.

The final wave

Changed

Razz: the first nine waves are easy and the final is insane because everyone spawns at once. The size was never the problem. The pace was.

  • In a mass assault a runner turned into a man every 0.2 seconds, against 0.45 in every other wave, and every trigger keyed on that count. The three charges, the car, the half-track, the teams and the Panzer all fired inside the first six seconds.
  • The final now comes in at the game’s own pace. Charges at about 6, 14 and 27 seconds instead of 2, 4 and 16, the Panzer at 28. The total, the cap and the forty runners on the skyline are unchanged.
  • Every soldier’s fire missions are ready as the final begins, and the break before it is 45 seconds.
  • In a soak from wave 10, a soldier with six men won the relief with the post at 83%. On Playtest 32 the same soldier died in the final. Single runs, not a verdict. Tell us.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Alt-tab out and back, and the music carries on where it was. A source the engine paused while the window was away was being taken for one that had finished, and restarted from the top. The break and citation music had the same fault. Please confirm on a real alt-tab; headless runs cannot lose focus.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. Everything here was checked with scripted runs and four-player soaks, and the pages were looked at in captures. Tell us what we missed.
  • On the pistol, the Modification slot’s pointer line crosses the SIGHTS line in the gunsmith. Readable, not cut.
  • A preset’s name is not yet shown on the after-action card.
  • Armour killed by a hired gunner still credits the host. STEADY CREWS covers the machine-gun crews only.
  • Crowd-control grenades (STUN, MOLOTOV, GAS as a loadout pick) are being built for the next build.

What we’d like you to test

  • Open the HQ on your main save and read the four lines. Did the RP come back? Which perks are you wearing now, and are they the ones you’d choose?
  • Pick three STORES caches and deploy. Were they there at wave 1? Try proposing one as a client.
  • Sign a HARDER preset and hold the front.
  • Play to the final wave with and without men. Does it still feel insane, or just hard?
  • Alt-tab at the HQ for a minute and come back.
  • Fit a Modification in the gunsmith and read FIELD SHEET.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

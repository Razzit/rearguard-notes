This one is about seeing how far you’ve come. You can see your progress on the road, after the run and at HQ. The Kar98 and the other sniper rifles land headshots again.

Back up your save before you play. It’s at %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Razz\Rearguard\save.json. This build adds new things to it, and Playtest 38 doesn’t know them. Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. A Playtest 38 build can’t join.

Sniper rifles

Changed

One of you said Kar98 headshots had got harder. You were right. Here’s what changed.

  • Hits follow the enemy’s body. When a man ducks, kneels or flinches, his head goes with him. A shot lands where you see it.
  • The Kar98k and the Lee-Enfield have thinner front posts. They don’t hide a head at range any more.
  • The five bolt and scoped rifles are dead accurate the moment you’re aimed in. Walking while aimed doesn’t add spread. Iron sights sway less.
  • Grenades measure their distance from the nearest part of a man, not his middle.

In the field

New
  • ADVANCE has a road along the top of the screen. Each post sits where it really is on the road, with the lorries at the end. A held post is green with a flag. The one in play pulses. You’re the diamond and your mates are the dots.
  • In DEFEND you get one pip per wave up to the relief, then a lorry.
  • The enemy left is a row of tally marks. They’re struck through in red as men fall. In a big counter-attack one mark can stand for 2 to 4 men.
  • Take a post and our flag goes up on it, with a green flare. Beat off a counter-attack and you get a flare too. Lose the post and the flag comes down.
  • The counter-attack clock is a bar on the road now.
  • Messages come one at a time, and a reward is never dropped.

After the run

New
  • THEN AND NOW. The after-action puts this deployment next to your first.
  • It draws the run. In ADVANCE you see the road post by post, with times and enemies stopped. In DEFEND you see each wave with its grade.
  • A stake marks the furthest you’ve got on the road. Get past it and you’ll see FURTHER THAN LAST TIME.
  • FIGURES breaks your pay into rows. A kill streak pays its top rung only. Objectives and DEFEND waves pay more.
  • A brand new soldier gets 400 RP extra on his first after-action.

HQ

New
  • The camp round the command post grows as you go. A new save starts with a tent, a brew tin and two men. Tents, lorries, a cookhouse and your hired men come as you rank up and win fronts.
  • The camp keeps count. A TALLY board shows enemies stopped, posts taken and fronts won. A supply dump grows at 100, 500, 1,500, 4,000, 10,000 and 25,000 stopped.
  • Coming back to HQ, the view holds on the camp for a moment. Any key skips it.
  • MAIL CALL. One letter or telegram each time you come back. Some ask you to choose. Read them again in the WAR DIARY under RECORDS.
  • Taking a posting doesn’t empty the camp any more. THE COMPANY’s things stay standing. Its skills are still reset.
  • New saves start simpler. BRIEFING shows one front and DEPLOY until rank 3. More opens up as you rank.
  • On BRIEFING the fronts are strung along one road.

ADVANCE

New

These open up once your save has won one ADVANCE.

  • 3 new objective kinds. BLOW IT: set charges. AMBUSH: hit a column on the road. RELIEVE: reach a section that’s been cut off.
  • Something can happen on the walk-on. A STRAFING RUN down the road, a SUPPLY DROP off the road, or men under a WHITE FLAG. Take them within 5 m. Some of them are lying.
  • Each front’s last objective has its boss in a tank, about 25 s into the hold.
  • The Ardennes and Henderson Field roads have their own rises, banks and fords now.

Hits

Changed
  • Enemies flinch hard from the side you hit, and stop firing for a moment. Rifles and MGs hit harder than pistols and SMGs.
  • Bigger hit puffs. Dust off the tunic and a red mist, more for a headshot.
  • The dead fall along the line of the shot and drop their rifle. Bodies stay until the walk-on.

From your reports

Changed
  • The Heavy Gunner has his own look: a bigger helmet, a darker tunic and crossed belts of rounds.
  • One bolt-rifle headshot kills an MG nest’s gunner.
  • Counter-attacks come in on a wider front.
  • The rifle grenade has a 50, 100 and 150 m range ladder.
  • The Z start-now line is easier to see.
  • Hedge banks don’t float. Snipers’ nests can see the wall.
  • The Ambush and Forward Observer cards say what they do more plainly.

Known issues

  • Nobody has played this build yet. Tell me what I missed.
  • The version line on the menu still says 0.38.1.
  • Someone who joins late doesn’t see bodies from before they joined.
  • The Ardennes road takes about 7 s to build when the map loads.
  • The letters and the other new words are first drafts.

What to test

  • Take the Kar98 out. Do your headshots land now? Try a man behind a wall and a man who’s just been hit.
  • Watch the road and the tally marks. Can you tell how far you’ve got and how many are left?
  • Come back to HQ a few times. Does the camp feel like it’s growing? Read your mail.
  • Start a new save and tell me if the start is clearer.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong.

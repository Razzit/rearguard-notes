Latest notes

The Japanese army

New

Henderson Field fights a different enemy with the same roles.

Infantry

New soldiers

Arisakas with long bayonets, Type 100 SMGs, pole-charge sappers, Type 96 LMG heavies, and officers who draw a katana to signal a charge.

Armour

Ha-Go and Chi-Ha

Light and medium tanks take the armoured car and Panzer’s places.

New event

Banzai charge

Announced, loud, and fast. Hold the line.

Pickups

Their weapons

Type 100, Type 99 LMG, scoped Type 97 and more.

Henderson Field

New

A fifth front: a Pacific jungle perimeter. The hardest one yet.

  • Fronts now name the enemy you’ll face at HQ.
  • The Tank Destroyer decoration counts either boss tank.

Co-op

Changed
  • Up to six soldiers per online section. The HUD stacks for big squads.
  • Teammates now visibly carry the gun actually in their hands.
  • A client who leaves mid-run banks their share once.
  • Clients can earn Squad Leader from the host’s squad.
  • Supply crates stay on the field until opened, through the wave break.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Ready-up works reliably in co-op.
  • Teammate markers show where your friends are.
  • The mouse cursor appears at the quartermaster’s wagon.
  • Downed soldiers drop off the MG nest instead of manning it.
  • F prefers the closest useful thing when several prompts overlap.
  • A NEW BEST stamp on the results when you beat your record.

What we asked you to test

  • Henderson Field: is it hard in a fun way or just hard?
  • Six players: does the HUD hold up? Does the post feel crowded?
  • Did the first-round fixes land?

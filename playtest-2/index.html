Japan Front Co-op Fixes Testing
Latest notes

The Japanese army

New

On Henderson Field you fight a different enemy. The roles are the same.

Infantry

New soldiers

They carry Arisakas with long bayonets and Type 100 SMGs. Sappers use pole charges, heavies carry the Type 96 LMG, and officers draw a katana to signal a charge.

Armour

Ha-Go and Chi-Ha

A light and a medium tank stand in for the armoured car and the Panzer.

New event

Banzai charge

You get a warning, then it comes in loud and fast.

Pickups

Their weapons

You can pick up the Type 100, the Type 99 LMG, the scoped Type 97 and more.

Henderson Field

New

The fifth front is a perimeter in the Pacific jungle. It’s the hardest one yet.

  • At HQ, each front now tells you which enemy you’ll face.
  • The Tank Destroyer decoration counts either boss tank.

Co-op

Changed
  • An online section now takes up to six soldiers. The HUD stacks for big squads.
  • Teammates are now shown carrying the gun they actually have in hand.
  • A client who leaves mid-run banks their share once.
  • Clients can earn Squad Leader from the host’s squad.
  • Supply crates stay on the field until someone opens them, even through the wave break.

Fixes

Fixed
  • Ready-up works reliably in co-op.
  • Teammate markers show where your friends are.
  • The mouse cursor appears at the quartermaster’s wagon.
  • Downed soldiers drop off the MG nest instead of manning it.
  • F prefers the closest useful thing when several prompts overlap.
  • The results show a NEW BEST stamp when you beat your record.

What we asked you to test

  • Henderson Field: is it hard in a fun way or just hard?
  • Six players: does the HUD hold up? Does the post feel crowded?
  • Did the first-round fixes land?

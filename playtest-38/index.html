Playtest 38 is the biggest change in a while. There’s a second way to play now. In ADVANCE you go forward instead of holding one post. I played it through first and fixed what I found before sending it out.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. A Playtest 37 build can’t join. Your save loads as it is, and your Grease Gun progress moves to the PPSh-41.

DEFEND or ADVANCE

New

You pick the mode at the BRIEFING, before you deploy. Press Tab there to swap.

  • DEFEND is the wave defence you know. Nothing about it changed, apart from one card pick per break.
  • ADVANCE is new. A new save starts on it.
  • In co-op, everyone plays the host’s mode.
  • Each mode keeps its own records.

ADVANCE

New

You take 4 objectives up a road. Take each one, then hold it against their counter-attack, then walk on to the next.

  • At the fourth you hold until the lorries come up the road. Get aboard and you’ve won.
  • A lorry brings up your guns and hired men once you’ve taken a post.
  • There’s no ready-up between objectives. Move up when you’re ready. The attack starts when they see you or you open fire.
  • The first objective’s marksman waits for the counter-attack. He can’t hit you from past 110 m, and his hits are capped.
  • Take a post late, while its counter-attack is already on, and everything they have holds off for about 20 s so you can dig in.
  • The fourth objective is sometimes a line of guns. Set a charge on each one with F. Their men keep coming the whole time, so don’t take too long about it.
  • ADVANCE has its own HUD, briefing, announcer lines, end screen and field manual page. You get PERFECT TAKE and PERFECT HOLD instead of PERFECT WAVE.
  • The ground on Hedgerow Farm rises and falls now. Banks, sunken lanes, knolls and hollows. The other fronts are still flat. They’re next.

Guns

Changed
  • The PPSh-41 replaces the Grease Gun. It fires very fast and empties a 35-round magazine in about 2 seconds. Your Grease Gun’s level and parts carry over. The 71-round drum is a part you unlock.
  • The Mosin-Nagant replaces the Springfield, with its own Soviet parts.
  • Most guns are new models, and you aim through their own iron sights. The Kar98k’s sights match the real rifle now: a small V, a stout post and a round hood.
  • Paint finishes colour the new models too.
  • The flamethrower, grenades, mortar and Luger have new models.
  • The MP40 you pick up used to shoot high of its sights. Fixed.

Sound

Changed
  • Bullets crack past you. Near misses snap, crack or whip by depending on how close they come.
  • Guns far away sound far away, and their sound arrives late.
  • Recorded gunshots, MG42, Luger and impacts.
  • You hear your rounds hit a man. A thud each time, a heavier one for a headshot or a kill. It still carries at 150 m. The Hit sounds option turns it off.

Fronts

Changed
  • Henderson Field: vehicles, bunkers and aircraft are Japanese now. Bamboo fences. Kunai grass hides a crouched man, both ways. The start line is 20 m nearer, with cover.
  • Ardennes: the road runs through standing timber, with rides and firebreaks. Shells burst in the treetops.
  • Factory District: brighter skies, so enemies are easier to pick out.
  • Monte Lungo: walls follow the slope properly.

Smaller changes

Changed
  • “MORTAR STONK” is “MORTAR STRIKE” now.
  • One card pick per break in DEFEND and in HOLD THE LINE.
  • The grade stamp on the end screen has its own corner and is easy to read.
  • Markers aren’t drawn inside your sight picture any more.
  • Co-op is steadier: a longer network timeout and a smoother return to HQ.

Known issues

  • Only I have played this build so far. Tell me what I missed.
  • If a player or the host crashes, the game takes about a minute to notice.
  • If the sound crackles, launch with -dspbuffer 1024.
  • The Ardennes tree bursts are sound and smoke only for now. They don’t hurt anyone.

What to test

  • Play ADVANCE on every front. Does moving up to the next objective feel right? Does the attack start when you expect?
  • Try the PPSh-41 and the Mosin-Nagant. Do your rounds land where the sights are?
  • Listen. Can you hear your hits? Do near misses and far guns sound right?
  • Play DEFEND too and tell me if anything broke there.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong.

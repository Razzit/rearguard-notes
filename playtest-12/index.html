Latest notes

Explosions

Changed
  • Blasts respect walls. Cover now protects you.
  • Shell shock scales with distance and cover.
  • Tripwire charges from the supply wagon.

Chat and ready-up

Changed
  • Read-aloud is on by default, cleaned and capped. Your own lines are read too.
  • Legendary pickups are announced in chat.
  • A ready panel in the break shows who is ready, in place of the count beside the timer.

Guns

Changed
  • Master Armourer replaces One Man Army as a Legendary.
  • Plain-steel sights under a weapon finish, so camo never hides the post.
  • Cartridges show for big rifle magazines.

Monte Lungo

Fixed
  • You can stand on the church roof; it’s out of the enemy’s reach but in their sight.
  • The belfry is shot at, not shelled.
  • Door thresholds no longer flicker.

MG siting

Fixed
  • MG42 teams set up where they can see the wall.
  • .30 cal pits slide clear of scenery and keep a clear line of fire.

What we asked you to test

  • Is read-aloud welcome on by default, or should it go back off?
  • Tripwire charges: worth the requisition?

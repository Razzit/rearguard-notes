Build your base

New
  • UPGRADES > BASE is now BUILD YOUR BASE. You spend points, not RP, and you can change your plan at HQ before any deployment. Thanks to Lachie for the mock-up, and Duncan for asking for a base you can see.
  • It opens at rank 9, with 11 points. You get one more a rank, up to 20 at rank 18. From rank 18 you can buy up to 5 more with RP.
  • If you bought base upgrades, you get all that RP back, and your old base is set up as your first plan.
  • Each gun has its own weapon and ammo. Weapons: the .30 cal (free), water-cooled (1 point), M2 .50 cal (2). Ammo: standard (free), Heavy Ball, Armour-piercing or Incendiary (2 each). Traversing Stop is 1 point a gun.
  • Each gate has its own wire. The first row is free. A second row is 1 point.
  • For the whole base: Taller Sandbags (2), Thicker Wire (2: men in wire move 45% slower, not 30%), Battery Allotment (4: +3 shells a barrage).
  • Three new upgrades. Emergency Flare (8): once a deployment, at 35% post health, every soldier’s fire mission is ready. Danger Close (4): fire missions can land inside the wire, and the shells can still hurt you. Radio Jamming (5): enemy mortars and rockets land twice as wide.
  • Layouts are free now: Standard Camp, Forward Pits and Double Ring.
  • In co-op the host’s plan is the one you fight with. You can see it on BRIEFING at any rank, but you can’t change it.
  • The campaign now opens at rank 10, so rank 9 brings one new thing.

Ready and E

Changed
  • Ready up is on Z now, or Enter. E is only your class kit and repair. A tap of E in a break no longer readies you.
  • Z still uses a field kit in a wave. The press that starts a wave won’t waste it.
  • Z does nothing for the first second of a break, so you won’t ready up by accident.
  • If you set your own keys, they stay. If something else already held Z, ready is on Enter and WHAT’S NEW tells you. You can change it in OPTIONS > CONTROLS.

Weapons rack

New
  • There’s a rack on the post wall, by the door. Look at a peg and hold F to leave a weapon there or take it back. That gets you more storage, and you can save your best gun for later.
  • Two pegs, yours alone. It holds bazookas, flamethrowers, picked-up guns, Panzerfausts and magnetic mines, one of each.
  • It keeps what’s left in it until the run ends. Nothing on the rack refills.
  • It works in a wave too. The hold is longer: 1.5 s, against under half a second in a break.
  • You still carry one heavy weapon. Take a heavy off a peg while carrying the other and they swap.
  • Your loadout guns, found guns with traits and a gunner’s issued StG can’t go on it. If you leave the game, the rack is empty when you come back.

Runs and breaks

Changed
  • You can take today’s conditions off a front. Where a front rolled some, CHANGE RUN has a NO CONDITIONS card under run A. You give up the pay they’d bring, and the front’s own bonus still counts. In co-op it’s the host’s pick.
  • A vote in a break adds 6 seconds, so reading it doesn’t use up your time for the card.
  • Creeping Barrage is gone. It was loud and gave you nothing to do. The Rolling Barrage fire plan, with its assault behind it, is still in.
  • When you take HOLD THE LINE, the banner says the relief is moving on, and they march off the field instead of vanishing halfway.

Machine gunner

Changed
  • Cool Hand: MG nests heat 50% slower. It was 20%.
  • Final Protective Fire: 10 seconds without heating. It was 6.
  • Captured Arms: the StG 44 your kit issues hits for 36, up from 30, and fights at your primary’s gun level. An StG off the ground is unchanged.
  • Tripod with the Feed Tray: the belt only feeds after half a second braced, so a quick crouch is no longer a free reload. It only slows that trick. Whether a braced gun should have unlimited ammo is still open, so tell us.

Fixes

Fixed
  • The medic’s posts stand on the ground and no longer float. The Aid Post and Casualty Clearing Post now show their reach with red-topped stakes and tape.
  • The downed pilot’s box no longer says you carry him. You walk him in, and he walks himself.
  • The report form has a real cursor. Arrow keys, Home, End and Delete all work, and Ctrl moves or deletes by the word.
  • A long report with blank lines no longer gets cut off in Discord.
  • Descriptions say “x% more damage” everywhere, not “hit x% harder”.
  • The M1911’s spread settles faster: 0.25 s, down from 0.4 s.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, with up to 4 soldiers in the field. The base points and upgrades have had no balance testing at all, so expect some to be too strong or too weak. The .50 with armour-piercing belts is the one we’d watch.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op. We haven’t found the cause. If it happens to you, please press F10 at HQ straight away, before you restart.
  • We couldn’t reproduce the captured StG having only one magazine, or vehicles getting stuck on Monte Lungo. If you see either, press F10 while it’s happening. For a stuck vehicle, the host’s report helps most.
  • With the HUD at 130%, the prompt for placing works runs a little past its box.
  • On concrete posts the rack sits in front of the open door leaf. It looks fine to us. Tell us if it doesn’t to you.
  • BRIEFING’s PLAY WITH FRIENDS panel has a small grey “Steam off” line in this build. It’s for our own testing. Ignore it. Join codes work without Steam.

What we’d like you to test

  • Spend your base points. Could you tell what each thing does? Is there one pick you’d always take, or one you’d never touch?
  • Use Z for a few runs. Does it feel right after a while? Did you ever ready up by accident, or lose a field kit?
  • Use the rack in a hard wave. Could you find it, and was the hold worth it? Did you save anything for later?
  • Pick NO CONDITIONS. Is it clear what you give up?
  • Answer a vote. Did you have time to read it and pick your card?
  • Play the gunner. Is the captured StG worth taking now? Is the tripod still too strong?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

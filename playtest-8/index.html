Latest notes

Battalion HQ

New
Rank 26

Five facilities

Armoury bench, Range, Radio room, Officers’ mess and Motor pool. Each has three levels.

Bottomless

Battalion Fund

Donate RP for a citation and a requisition chit every 25,000.

Veteran Points

Veteran traits

Five traits, like Steady Nerves and Sharpshooter. You wear one at a time and can swap for free.

Cosmetics

New

There are 54 to earn and none to buy. They include weapon-mastery finishes, gold Master Gilt, Honours camos, helmet stencils, calling cards and officer’s kit. The Colonel’s eagle comes at rank 50. Teammates see what you wear.

Dispatches and dossiers

New
  • Dispatches: short-term tasks between deployments.
  • You collect intelligence dossiers on the enemy you face.
  • Decorations: 16 → 41.

Economy pass

Changed
  • Everything you spend RP on costs more now, so progress lasts. Battalion facilities cost 1000 / 2500 / 5000 ×4.
  • Quartermaster’s Favour is +5% a level (was +8%).
  • Requisition chits can also re-roll the commendation cards. Once the free reroll is spent, they can re-roll a dispatch too.

Fixes

Fixed
  • A wave can no longer hang on one stuck straggler or the last vehicle.
  • A downed or killed soldier’s name stops short of the DOWN / KIA stamp.

What we asked you to test

  • Do the long-term goals give you a reason to come back?
  • Prices: does anything feel out of reach?

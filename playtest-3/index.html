Latest notes

Pings

New
Q

Mark it

Ping what you’re looking at, whether it’s an enemy, a gun or a crate. Your team sees it on screen and on the compass.

Z

Here

Call your team to where you stand.

Stacked

Busy spots

When pings sit side by side, their labels stack instead of overlapping.

Roll call

New
  • Hold Tab for the roll call. It shows every soldier’s kills, downs, revives and requisition this run.
  • The after-action telegram gets a roll call section too.

HQ lobby

New
  • Everyone gathers at HQ before the host deploys. The host can kick a player.
  • You can’t deploy an online session while it’s still opening.

Key rebinding

New
  • Options › CONTROLS lists every action. Click one and press a key.
  • A key that’s already in use swaps with the old binding. Esc stays the pause key.
  • On-screen prompts now name the key you actually bound.

What we asked you to test

  • Pings: are they readable mid-fight? Too many, too few?
  • Rebinding: anything you couldn’t rebind that you wanted to?

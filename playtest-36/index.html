This one is the skill tree. Each class has its own small tree now, and THE COMPANY is a tree everyone shares. We built a version of this as Playtest 35, played it ourselves, found the skills flat and never sent it out. So this is the second go. This time every skill is meant to change what you do or see in a fight.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads, and anything that was removed is paid back in RP.

The skill tree

New

It lives under UPGRADES > TREE and replaces the old class pages. It opens on a hub with six cards: one for each class and one for THE COMPANY. Each card shows how far along you are and what’s next.

  • Each tree fits on one page. No zooming or panning. Q and E step between the trees and Esc takes you back to the hub.
  • Rank opens a skill and RP buys it. A skill that glows is one you can take now. Hover for its card and hold the mouse button to take it.
  • Each class tree has 13 skills. Your class kit sits at the root, then a line of skills to the capstone at rank 40. There’s a fork where you pick one of two sides, and a bag of extra kits.
  • Your guns are on the tree too. They sit on THE RACK along the foot of every page, at the same ranks and prices as before.
  • Hold Tab to see the name of every skill on the page.
  • In co-op the hub shows which class everyone in the lobby is playing.
  • Four skills are marked IN TRAINING. You can see them but can’t buy them yet: Captured MG42, Fire and Movement, Sappers and Second Kit.

The classes

New

A class’s skills only work when you deploy as that class. The exception is the Field Skill slot on THE COMPANY, which lets you wear one skill with a small flag from another class’s tree.

  • Rifleman. Rifle and Carbine kills add notches to your tally, and a headshot adds 2. At 5, press E for Mad Minute: 6 seconds of fast fire with no reloading. At 3 or 4 notches E fires a rifle grenade where you look. Later skills let your rounds go through a man, through hedges, and drop anyone climbing the sandbags with one shot. Rapid Fire, Lads! gets the whole section firing flat out during your Mad Minute.
  • Gunner. Long Burst is your kit now. Press E and for 8 seconds your gun needs no reload and never overheats, and every man you hit is pinned. Two a wave. Press R at an overheated nest to change the barrel in 2 seconds. Fire Lane and Grazing Fire pin men your bursts pass near. With Nobody Crosses at rank 40, a man you keep pinned can’t move at all.
  • Medic. Toss the Tin throws a syrette to anyone in your sights up to 12 m away. Shout Him Up gets a downed man up from 15 m without you leaving your wall. With Fight from the Ground you keep shooting while you’re down, and every kill puts 9 seconds back on your clock. While a medic with Nobody Left Behind is on his feet, nobody’s bleed-out clock runs.
  • Engineer. Trip mines are back on H. Assault Pioneer puts 2 satchel charges in your grenade pouch every wave. The other side of that fork, Working Party, puts a squadmate on each of your 2 newest walls. There’s a Fougasse that shoots fire 25 m, foxholes you dig in the break, and the Clacker: hold E and everything you laid in view goes up at once.
  • Radioman. In the binoculars, tap X to pick BLAST, SMOKE or FIRE shells. Harassing Fire takes the supply drop’s place: stake a spot and a shell lands on it every 15 seconds till the wave ends. Ping during a barrage and the shells walk to your ping. Fire Plan at rank 40 lets you mark 3 spots each break, and each one fires a free barrage in the wave.

THE COMPANY

Changed

THE COMPANY’s skills work whatever class you deploy as. This is also where your damage and health come from now.

  • Every soldier handles like a veteran from day one. Faster aim, fire and reloads, bigger magazines, and 120 health. You used to earn that slowly through Service Conditioning.
  • Rank doesn’t add damage or health any more. Four cheap Company skills do: Stopping Power at rank 5, Hardened at 10, Old Soldier at 18 and Man-Stopper at 19. Hardened and Old Soldier make the command post tougher too.
  • If you were already past those ranks, they’re yours already. You’re exactly as strong as you were.
  • Covering Fire, Lads! at rank 17. When you’re pinned, the 2 nearest squadmates fire on the men pinning you for 4 seconds.
  • Old Sweats gets your squadmates reviving each other, and under fire they keep shooting instead of ducking.
  • Grenadier’s Pouch carries a second kind of grenade and 2 more grenades. Hold G and press F to swap.
  • The card skills: A Set Is Yours makes one card set come up 3 times as often, Fourth Card shows 4 cards in every offer, and Starting Card gives you a pick before wave 1.
  • Squad training and the post’s walls now come from the best soldier in your section, not the host’s rank.
  • The easier Replacements difficulty is gone. New soldiers are strong enough without it.

Your soldier and your save

Changed
  • Your guns came across as they were. So did most kits.
  • Some kits changed. The Gunner’s kit is Long Burst instead of the Ammunition Box. The Rifleman’s tally ends in Mad Minute. The Radioman’s supply drop is Harassing Fire now. The whistle is Come On, Then!
  • Old kit upgrades are part of their kits now, for free, and the RP you paid for them is back.
  • Every skill that was removed is paid back in full in RP. A card at BRIEFING tells you once what came across and how much RP you got.
  • Veteran Points are gone. Each one you hadn’t spent was turned into 60,000 RP, and the field commission at rank 50 pays 60,000 RP now.
  • The rank bonuses from rank 33 to 49 stay as they were.

A new posting

New

Once you own the last skill of any class tree, you can take A NEW POSTING from the bar at the foot of the hub. It’s optional, and it can’t be undone. You type POST to confirm.

  • Every class tree and THE COMPANY are wiped, with no refund. The 4 health and damage skills on THE COMPANY stay.
  • You keep your rank, RP, guns, squad and base. You also keep 1 wiped skill of your choice.
  • Each posting adds +30% RP to every deployment after it. Five postings is the most, for +150%.
  • Postings I and III give you an extra Field Skill slot. Posting II makes each class’s first skill and both sides of every fork free. From posting IV, THE COMPANY is never wiped. At V, the last skill of each tree costs half.

The look and the words

Changed
  • The tree pages look like the design we drew. A dark map table, and every skill has its own picture.
  • A finished tree says so, even with IN TRAINING skills left on it. Those are taped off now.
  • RELOADING and COOKING no longer hide behind your cards. They sit beside the card strip.
  • The bottom-right corner with your grenades and kit is readable on bright ground now.
  • Long messages wrap instead of running into the command post title.
  • We rewrote about 2,200 lines of the game’s text in plainer words, so it should make sense the first time you read it. If something still reads oddly, press F10 and tell us.
  • Commendations are called cards now.
  • The field manual has 8 new notes: being pinned, Spirit, postings, Honours, the Pact, HOLD THE LINE, shock troopers and sergeants. Each one comes up the first time it matters.

Known issues

  • Nobody outside the team has played this build. It was checked with scripted runs and six-player soaks, one player for each class. Tell us what we missed.
  • You carry one kit on E. If a Rifleman carries Sniper’s Veil, Dummy Head or Come On, Then!, he can’t use Mad Minute or the rifle grenade. It’s the same for the Gunner’s Long Burst, the Radioman’s flares and the Medic’s syrettes. Second Kit is the fix, and it’s still IN TRAINING.
  • Some skills are still thin, mostly the Gunner from rank 14 to 33, the Engineer from 30 to 40 and the Medic’s Field Surgeon side. Tell us which ones you never notice.
  • Covering Fire, Lads! fires back on nearly every pin, but it rarely lifts the pin.
  • Shout Him Up does much the same as Field Surgeon.
  • Your reports from Playtest 34 aren’t in this build. They’re next, in Playtest 37.

What we’d like you to test

  • Play each class to its fork. Which side did you pick, and did you feel it in the fight?
  • Look at the card at BRIEFING the first time you load. Does the RP you got back look right?
  • Try a Field Skill. Wear a skill from another class and see if it earns the slot.
  • Tell us the skills you never noticed. That’s the list we work from next.
  • If you get a tree finished, have a look at A NEW POSTING. You don’t have to take it.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

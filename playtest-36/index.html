The skill tree

New

UPGRADES > TREE opens on a page with six cards: one for each class and one for THE COMPANY. Click a card to open that tree.

  • Your rank unlocks a skill, then you buy it with RP. A skill that glows is one you can buy now. Hover over it to read what it does. Hold the mouse button on it to buy it.
  • Q and E switch between trees. Esc goes back to the six cards.
  • Hold Tab to see the name of every skill.
  • Each class tree starts with your class kit. Partway along you pick one of two skills. You can’t have both.
  • Your guns are along the bottom of every tree page. Same ranks and prices as before.
  • In co-op the first page shows which class everyone is playing.
  • Four skills are taped off. You can see them but can’t buy them yet: Captured MG42, Fire and Movement, Sappers and Second Kit.

The classes

New

A class’s skills only work when you deploy as that class.

  • Rifleman. Rifle and Carbine kills fill a counter. A headshot counts 2. At 3 or 4, press E to fire a rifle grenade. At 5, press E for 6 seconds of fast fire with no reloading. Later skills let your shots go through hedges and through an enemy into the one behind.
  • Gunner. Press E and for 8 seconds your gun doesn’t reload or overheat, and every enemy you hit is pinned. Twice a wave. Press R on an overheated gun to change the barrel in 2 seconds.
  • Medic. You can throw a syrette to a teammate up to 12 m away. You can get a downed teammate up from 15 m away. While you’re down you can keep shooting, and each kill gives you more time before you bleed out.
  • Engineer. Trip mines are back on H. You can get 2 satchel charges every wave, dig foxholes in the break, and set off everything you’ve laid at once.
  • Radioman. In the binoculars, tap X to pick BLAST, SMOKE or FIRE shells. You can mark a spot and a shell lands on it every 15 seconds until the wave ends.

THE COMPANY

Changed

THE COMPANY skills work whatever class you deploy with.

  • New soldiers start stronger. 120 health, faster aiming, firing and reloading, and bigger magazines.
  • Your damage and health now come from four cheap skills in THE COMPANY, at ranks 5, 10, 18 and 19. Rank on its own doesn’t add them any more. If you’re already past those ranks, you have them already, and you’re as strong as before.
  • At rank 17: when you’re pinned, your 2 nearest squadmates shoot at whoever is pinning you.
  • One skill lets your squadmates revive each other.
  • One skill lets you carry a second kind of grenade and 2 more grenades. Hold G and press F to swap.
  • Three skills change the upgrade cards: one set comes up more often, you see 4 cards instead of 3, and you get a pick before wave 1.
  • One slot lets you use a skill from another class’s tree.
  • The Replacements difficulty is gone. New soldiers don’t need it now.

Your save

Changed
  • Your guns came across as they were. So did most kits.
  • Some kits changed. The Gunner’s ammunition box, the Rifleman’s tally and the Radioman’s supply drop work differently now. See the classes above.
  • Anything removed is paid back in full in RP. The first time you load, a card at BRIEFING shows what came across and how much RP you got back.
  • Veteran Points are gone. Each unspent one became 60,000 RP. The rank 50 reward now pays 60,000 RP.

Starting over for more RP

New

Once you finish a whole class tree, you can choose to start your trees over for more RP. It’s optional and it can’t be undone. It’s called A NEW POSTING, at the bottom of the first tree page.

  • All your skills are wiped, with no refund, except the 4 damage and health skills. You keep your rank, RP, guns, squad and base, plus 1 skill you choose.
  • Each time you do it, you earn 30% more RP from then on. You can do it up to 5 times.
  • Each time also gives you a small bonus, like an extra slot or cheaper skills.

Text and screens

Changed
  • I did a pass on all the text in the game. It should make more sense now. If something still reads oddly, press F10 and tell me.
  • Commendations are called cards now.
  • Every skill has its own picture.
  • RELOADING and COOKING no longer hide behind your cards.
  • Your grenades and kit in the bottom-right corner are easier to read on bright ground.
  • The field manual has 8 new tips. Each one shows up the first time it matters.

Known issues

  • Nobody outside the team has played this build yet. Tell me what I missed.
  • You can only carry one kit on E. If you pick a different kit from your tree, you lose your class’s main E ability. For example, a Rifleman with Dummy Head can’t use the 6 seconds of fast fire. Second Kit will fix this. It’s still taped off.
  • Some skills are hard to notice in a fight. Tell me which ones.
  • Your reports from Playtest 34 aren’t in this build. They’re coming in Playtest 37.

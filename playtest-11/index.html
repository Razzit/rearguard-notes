Latest notes

Hit feedback

Changed
  • You get an X hitmarker on every hit.
  • New hit sounds. They’re lower and softer.
  • Suppression is lighter, with more ways to shake it off.

Hidden spawns

New

Half the enemy squads now come in where no defender can see them. Keep checking your flanks.

Threat pings

Changed
  • Threat pings are smaller on the compass. They stay up for 4.5 s at most and go away when the threat is dealt with.
  • Supply crate pings stay up until the crate is opened (it takes about 12 s to land).

Commendations and the wagon

Changed
  • Commendations wait to be picked in the break. The cards never pause or carry into the wave.
  • The commendation tab is easier to read mid-fight.
  • The wagon keeps its upgrades: a standing issue plus delivery.
  • Borrow is gone from the wagon.

What we asked you to test

  • Hidden spawns: does it feel sneaky, or unfair?
  • The new hitmarker and sounds: better or worse?

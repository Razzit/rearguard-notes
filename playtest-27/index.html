Patch Notes

Recut, late on 3 November. The first cut went out that afternoon; the recut below is what’s in the build now. The WHAT’S NEW card comes up again so you see it.

PLAYTEST
27

This one is the HQ. It’s been redrawn from scratch as a field desk. Four tabs, one page each, and your soldier stands on the page with his gun and his kit. The old HQ had grown a lot of panels over the last ten playtests. We also cut the game down for new soldiers, so its systems open one at a time, and we fixed eight things you reported from playtest 26.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this build. An older build is turned away when it tries to join. Your save loads as it is. Nothing is wiped or refunded.

The HQ is a field desk

Changed
  • Every page is one sheet of paper. One hand of type, in two sizes. What you can press is a button. What you own is a card. The tab you’re on has the red edge.
  • The top bar is the same on every page. Your rank and RP on the left, then MANUAL, WHAT’S NEW, REPORT, OPTIONS and QUIT. The yellow slip in the middle tells you what the page is for.
  • Fewer things are behind a modal. Lists are lists. The things you can’t afford yet are hidden, not greyed out, so a page only shows what you can do today.
  • The old HQ is still in the build. Start the game with -oldhq on the command line, or in Steam under Launch Options, and you get it back. If you need to do that, tell us which page sent you there.

The four tabs

Changed
  • BRIEFING shows the front as a map, with the enemy’s arrows on it. The fronts you can pick are down the left side. Under the map is your kit strip and what the next deployment pays. DEPLOY is the big red stamp, bottom right. CHANGE RUN is off the first page. Fewer things to read before you go.
  • LOADOUT has three pages: WEAPONS, SPECIALIST and APPEARANCE. Your soldier stands in the middle with his gun on his back. The class patch is on his sleeve, and lines run from him to what he’s carrying. The guns are a gallery, ten of them with their parts in one pass. A FIELD SHEET view lists everything in one column, if you’d rather read.
  • UPGRADES keeps the soldier on the page and hangs the offers on him. A card you can take says DRAW, and its tier is the coloured chip. SQUAD shows the man you can hire in a ghost tint. BASE is here too, see below.
  • RECORDS is your service record. The fronts are a strip across the top. Dispatches show their pips only once you’ve made progress on one. Commendations are field cards now, the same cards you see in the break. Your archive is at the bottom. Credits moved under OPTIONS.
  • In a co-op game the host plans the base and picks the front. A client sees THE HOST’S FRONT and THE HOST’S BASE, with nothing to press, and marks ready.
  • At 1280×720 with 130% text, every line fits. That was the size that broke most often, so we checked all of it there.

A leaner game

Changed
  • Things open by rank. A new soldier sees a plain game first and meets each system at the rank it opens. Anything that opened since your last visit wears NEW on its tab.
  • Veterans keep everything. Your rank, guns and unlocks are all there, and everything your rank has opened is on the page from your first visit. We hid things, we didn’t delete them.
  • Recut: the first cut paced this by visits and deployments, so a rank-18 soldier found SQUAD and BASE missing. That’s gone. If you played the first cut, your next visit shows everything your rank opens, without NEW on all of it.
  • The fight is quieter. Fewer banners and call-outs. The order card, the clock and the place are the biggest things on the screen.
  • Each break is for one thing. You pick your card. The works build themselves.
  • Pay is one number. Tier and Pact are one heat ladder now.

Placing your base

New
  • BASE is now a plan you place things on. Nests and pits go where you put them, on the post’s drawing, with the keys shown as caps. Points come from rank, as in playtest 25. If you can’t afford an option today, it isn’t shown.
  • In co-op the host places. Everyone sees the plan on their BRIEFING before the lorries leave.

Fixes from your reports

Fixed
  • Recut: the purple slab. Kill the relief boss fast, before he sets up, and the track he throws turned magenta. It keeps its steel now. If anything ever draws purple again, press F10: the report names it.
  • Recut: [Y] in a fight. A tap no longer opens every card to its full rule. Hold [Y]: the faces at once, the full rule after 0.8 s, gone when you let go. Rerolling with [Y] held keeps the fresh cards up. The break keeps its tap-to-read.
  • Recut: Work Party. The repair card comes when the post is hurt. With the post whole it’s a quarter as likely as before, at half health as likely as before, at a quarter health or less four times as likely.
  • Recut: the works window. Every third break it now says “[Hold F] Put the wire on the east side” and “Look at a side and hold [F]: the pioneers build there. Free. Leave it and they build it in 12 s.” A one-line caption explains it the first time.
  • Recut: WEAPONS. Clicking a gunsmith slot no longer swaps the gun’s part to the next one you could fit. It shows what you have until you hover or pick.
  • MG nests dig in where they fit. No more nests through a building or right beside you. We checked 288 sites on the five maps.
  • One prompt under the crosshair. If a crate lands at a gun, you see the crate’s prompt, not the gun’s.
  • Sniper’s Veil works at range. A hidden man isn’t picked out from far off, and no new hunt turns on him while he’s hidden.
  • E works after a ready tap. The ready key let go of its claim late, so your next E did nothing.
  • The pilot’s box says you walk him in. You never carried him.
  • The KEPT row on the after-action card wraps now. It was the last line still getting cut off.
  • The order barks match the clock. The old lines described the old jobs.
  • The licences file names the Steamworks SDK, and the CC0 text is in it.

Known issues

  • Nobody had played this build when these notes went up. We checked it with scripted runs, 1,247 screenshots of the HQ at two sizes, and sessions with 6 soldiers. A redrawn HQ will have things we didn’t see. Tell us the page and send a screenshot.
  • Your soldier faces front on every page. He doesn’t turn yet.
  • On a client’s SERVICE RECORD there’s an empty gap where RANGE used to be. RANGE is the host’s.
  • On BASE, options you can pick together still grey out when you can’t afford them, instead of hiding like the rest.
  • A dispatch you haven’t started shows a card with an empty lower half.
  • A few lone tree crowns on the Hedgerow Farm map could pass for ponds.
  • A tester was dropped to the menu a few times in co-op in playtest 26. We still haven’t found the cause. If it happens, press F10 at HQ straight away.

What we’d like you to test

  • Can you find things? Pick a front, change your gun, buy an upgrade, read your record. Tell us where you got lost, or where you had to guess.
  • Does the map help? Does BRIEFING tell you enough to pick a front, or did you miss the old list?
  • If you can, start a new soldier. Does the game open up at the right pace, or does it hide too much?
  • Host a game and place your base. Did your mates see the plan? Did the nests go where you put them?
  • Play at 1280×720 if you can, with text at 130%. Anything cut off is a bug.
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong. It sends the map code for you.

Changing class

Changed

You can change class from the hub or BRIEFING. It was there in Playtest 36, but hard to find.

  • UPGRADES > TREE: every class card you can play has a DEPLOY AS button. The class you play says DEPLOYING AS.
  • A class’s own tree page also shows DEPLOY AS at the top right, whatever skill you’re looking at.
  • BRIEFING: CHANGE CLASS on the class card takes you to those cards.
  • To play a class, buy its first skill on its tree. A card you can’t play yet says so.
  • The Field Skill you wear shows on the tree. Its flag turns orange with FIELD SKILL under it, Field Skill Slot shows which skill it is, and THE COMPANY card on the hub names it. If you play the class it comes from, it says the slot isn’t needed and you can take it off there.
  • Strongpoint’s +6 points only count when you play Engineer. Play another class and POST DEFENCES marks the guns that need them. Those guns deploy cut back to fit. Your plan stays saved for when you play Engineer again.

Medic

Fixed

More of the medic’s kit now works on your squadmates as well as other players. In co-op too, when the medic isn’t the host.

  • Rescue Smoke works on a downed squadmate. Look at him and press E: the prompt names him and the smoke lands on him.
  • The Shell Dressings and Smelling Salts cards work on squadmates. So does Blood Plasma: a squadmate you revive gets up at full health.
  • A syrette goes to a downed squadmate when you look at his body, even if his feet are behind a sandbag. Before, it went to you.
  • Field Surgeon and Shout Him Up already reached squadmates in Playtest 36. They still do.

Grenades

Changed
  • The stun grenade goes off 1.5 s after you press G, not 3.5 s. You can still hold it to cook it. If it goes off in the air, it stuns the ground below.
  • The molotov breaks on the first thing it hits. It can’t be cooked. Holding G only aims it. That includes our own sandbags and wire, so throw over them.
  • While you hold a grenade, the line under the crosshair shows that grenade’s own fuse.
  • Grenadier’s Pouch swaps on its own key: tap Alt. Holding G only cooks now, and F no longer swaps. You can swap before you throw or while you cook. Change the key in OPTIONS > CONTROLS.

Ammunition

Changed
  • The STORES ammunition box gives each player 2 full refills a deployment. One refill fills everything you carry: grenades, satchels, every magazine, rifle grenades, a flamethrower or bazooka, and a captured gun.
  • One hold of F is one refill. Before, one hold took both.
  • If you’re fully stocked, the box tells you and keeps your refill.
  • The Trench Gun reloads only the shells it needs. 1 or 2 shells take less than half the time of a full reload. Before, they took the full time. An empty gun takes as long as before. Faster reload skills speed up these short reloads too.

The HUD

Changed
  • Aim at a tank, armoured car, half-track or truck to see its health bar. It shows at the top under COMMAND POST while it’s in your crosshair, and for 2.5 s after. Hitting it starts the 2.5 s again. This is on the LEAN HUD, the one you start with. The FULL HUD (OPTIONS) shows every bar, as before.
  • In the break, the LEAN HUD no longer shows your card rules under the wave count. Hold Tab to see them.

The field

Fixed
  • You can shoot through the gaps of a Nebelwerfer to hit the rockets behind. Its wreck no longer has an invisible wall round it. A new Nebelwerfer sets up at least 10 m from an old wreck.
  • On the Factory’s clear day the far town no longer disappears into the sun’s glare. The dust is thinner. Other weather and other maps are the same.
  • Hold the Crossroads: stay in the ring for 30 s, down from 45 s. The first enemies come at it 9 s after the order.

Known issues

  • Nobody outside the team has played this build yet. Tell me what I missed.
  • Molotovs often break on our own sandbags and wire when you throw from behind them. Tell me if that gets in the way.
  • On the Factory’s clear day you may spot enemies sooner through the thinner dust.
  • You still carry one kit on E. Second Kit is still taped off.

What to test

  • Play a medic with squadmates. Do your syrettes, smoke and medic cards reach them? In co-op, try it when you’re not the host.
  • Throw a stun grenade and a molotov. Does the 1.5 s fuse feel right?
  • Got Grenadier’s Pouch? Swap with Alt. Does that key suit you?
  • Aim at armour. Do you see its health bar?
  • Press F10 the moment something looks wrong.
